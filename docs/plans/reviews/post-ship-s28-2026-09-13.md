### Verified clean (no finding)

Preview answers 402 before any confirm.
- **FY maths / legal** — `sydneyDate` uses `Intl.DateTimeFormat("en-CA", { timeZone: "Australia/Sydney" })`; the suite pins 30 Jun 23:59 AEST (13:59Z) in the closing FY and 1 Jul 00:30 AEST in the next; date-only strings pass through. Totals = `sumFyTotals(rows)` re-rounded to cents, `grossedUp = gross + credits`, `reconciled` flag checks franked + unfranked = gross, gross − TFN = net. Per-distribution table with payment date, statement number, franking %, company tax rate. Wording: "may be reported to the ATO by the company" (AIIR not asserted), holding-period caveat, "not tax advice" note on the payload and a s 766B / no-AFSL / not-a-registered-tax-agent footer on the PDF; the entity block is the founder's company, BlockID named only as the tool. PDF is `@react-pdf/renderer` primitives — no HTML sink.
