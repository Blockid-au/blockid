## Verdict

**GO — three P1 and two cheap P2 defects fixed on this branch; merge before the first `investor-followups` tick (21:00 UTC).** Security posture is sound: every mutating route is `apiRoute()`-wrapped, scope-gated at the right minimum role, keyed on the project OWNER (no IDOR oracle on `[roundId]` / `[commitmentId]` / `[recordId]` / `accessTokenId`), the cron is constant-time authorised and `?dry=1` writes nothing, emails escape every founder- and investor-typed string and `withFromName` cannot inject headers. The maths (share-price blend, commitment roll-up, term-sheet weights, acqui-hire waterfall) is correct. The three P1s were **money and silent-feature** defects, not exposure: a paid-but-empty data-room compile with no refund, a follow-up cron that would starve itself after 50 sends, and a `first_view` founder alert that could never fire in production. No new migration is needed; 0355 / 0356 / 0360 are idempotent and 0356 is 0351 plus the header and the 126th map row.
