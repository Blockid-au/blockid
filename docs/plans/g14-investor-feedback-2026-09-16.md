## 2. Target state (one paragraph each)

**Evaluator.** A program manager or angel screening committee buys Program / Cohort 25 / Fund, creates an intake link (`/apply/<slug>`), and every founder who applies lands in a scored inbox (SVI, coverage, dossier link, CSV) — the committee ranks its own top-10 first, then sees the SVI table. Their tools get the same data: `GET /api/v1/evaluations` with scoped keys, and assessments / feedback letters / intake submissions pushed to Slack, Affinity or Airtable. Every score carries an origin-capped evidence ladder, a "Verified ABN" badge when a reviewer has approved it, a public `/methodology` page and a published backtest (ρ, N, CI) they can put in front of a sponsor or IC.

**Founder.** Applies through a program's link with the data sentence in view, gets the free score, and — once ≥ 3 assessors from ≥ 2 organisations have recorded a verdict — receives a "What investors said" letter (ratings, risk buckets, questions; never who said it) that feeds their next-best action. Their execution profile (exits, raises, roles, full-time %, GitHub) drives FTV from a rubric they can see; requesting verification sends evidence to a reviewer queue rather than self-awarding the top tier; a weekly Δ mail explains any score move caused by the verification change.

**Trust.** Confidence is a function of origin, not keywords: founder input ≤ `document_uploaded`, connectors → `connected_source | transaction_data`, reviewer/admin only → `third_party_verified`; an L0–L5 verification multiplier (0.85–1.10) is recorded in `meta.verification`; `/methodology` states the 8 dimensions, evidence ladder, verification levels, audit chain, model provenance and versioning; `/methodology/calibration` publishes backtest v0 with survivorship caveats; open AU registers (ABR bulk, business.gov.au grants, R&DTI transparency) feed LCO/IRI/TRE evidence rows and a real cohort for percentiles. `/api/status.traction` proves the numbers on the deck every day.
