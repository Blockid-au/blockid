[Stripe tax behavior documentation](https://docs.stripe.com/tax/products-prices-tax-codes-tax-behavior).

Unspecified alone cannot pass. Bind the observed default and price identities to the witness; unknown/exclusive/stale/mismatched settings fail closed. Independently verify actual paid session economics against the advertised pack amount. Do not silently infer tax jurisdiction or repair accounting by changing amounts.

This observation is a current catalogue check, not proof of every historical checkout, tax registration, subscription SKU or transaction. No Stripe tax code, default or immutable price behavior was changed. Updating a price from unspecified to explicit inclusive would be a separate provider change; Stripe states explicit inclusive/exclusive behavior cannot subsequently be switched on that Price.
