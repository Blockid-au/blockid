# Biz Trust Report: investor screening and current information

Reviewed 25 September 2026. This document records research and the implementation
scope; operational release evidence is recorded separately. It extends G31/G32,
without claiming their remaining calibration and acceptance work is complete.

## Primary research

- [IPEV 2025 guidelines](https://www.privateequityvaluation.com/Portals/0/Documents/Guidelines/2025%20IPEV%20Valuation%20Guidelines.pdf), published December2025, applicable to quarterly reporting periods beginning on/after1April2026. Relevant sections:2.6 calibration,2.7 backtesting,3 valuation techniques,5.10 contractual rights,5.12 scenario analysis. Method selection and inputs require judgment; a recent transaction can calibrate a model but is not an automatic current valuation. AI assistance does not transfer accountability from the valuer. Application here: retain method-local inputs/derivations, distinguish missing from zero and require a reviewed financial producer before official valuation activation.
- [IVSC current standards](https://ivsc.org/standards/) and [2025 effective edition announcement](https://ivsc.org/new-edition-of-the-international-valuation-standards-ivs-published/): document scope, basis, approaches, inputs, models and reporting; IVS200 addresses business interests. Application here: show saved report date/version and limits, identify missing valuation information. No claim of IVS certification or full compliance. The2028 exposure draft is not treated as the operative standard.
- [CFA V(A)](https://www.cfainstitute.org/standards/professionals/code-ethics-standards/standards-of-practice-v-a): adequate research and a reasonable basis support analysis. [CFA V(B)](https://www.cfainstitute.org/standards/professionals/code-ethics-standards/standards-of-practice-v-b): distinguish facts from opinions and disclose analysis limitations. Application here: expose saved citations and assumptions, separate AI assessments from independent verification, and use further-diligence wording rather than an immediate-investment label.

These sources are valuation/research references, not a universal startup scoring
rubric. The six priorities below are our product interpretation of the existing
Biz Trust requirements. SVI remains a versioned BlockID index; this presentation
change does not certify or recalibrate its numerical method.

## Current information and truth fixes

Version metadata must distinguish package version, deployed release/source,
report schema, pipeline and prompt version. A UI change is not evidence that old
reports were regenerated using a new model. Team activity is a dated stored
snapshot, not an assertion of a current employee roster or live30-day output.

Platform statistics require metric definitions, source time and availability.
User accounts are not businesses; analyses are not unique completed companies.
Remove placeholder500monthly visitors, SVI×10000 monetary aggregates and zero
fallbacks on unavailable queries. Refresh the reviewed aggregate snapshot using
read-only database access; preserve exclusion rules and state whether analysis
row counts include QA/reruns. Do not expose customer records or credentials.
