## 1. Purpose

Judges at the 2026-09-16 pitch said the deck had no customer research and no clear buyer. This instrument answers, on the record: **who screens startups in Australia, what it costs them today, what a first-pass screen must produce, what makes an AI score trustworthy enough for a committee or sponsor, who signs a A$2,500–5,000/yr line item, and what they report upward after selection.** Outputs feed deck v3 (S6/S7/S11), pricing v4 (Fund / Intake link / Cohort), G14 sprint priorities (S34 feedback letter, S35 intake, S36 verification, S38 integrations) and the D30/D60/D90 rollup.

Format: 45 minutes, video or in person, one interviewer, notes typed live into the record table (no recording unless the person asks for one). Interviews are **not** sales calls — the pilot offer is mentioned only if the interviewee asks.

## 2. Consent line (read at the start, verbatim)

> "This is a 45-minute research conversation for BlockID.au. I will take notes and keep them anonymised — role, organisation type, city and date only, no names — and nothing you say will be quoted publicly, attributed or used in marketing without your written approval. You can stop at any time or ask me to strike anything. Are you happy to go ahead?"

Record the answer as `consent: yes/no` in the record table. If the answer is anything but a clear yes, thank them and end.

## 3. Anonymisation rule

- Each record is identified by **role · organisation type · city · date** — e.g. *screening-committee member · angel group · Sydney · 2026-09-23*. **Never** a person's name, the organisation's name, or a detail that identifies either (a unique program name, a portfolio company, a fund size only one group has).
- Verbatim quotes go in the record with `quote-approval: pending` and are used nowhere else until the person replies "approved" in writing; then `quote-approval: approved (date)`. Declined → `declined`; the quote stays in the record for internal use only.
- Contact details live in the founder's private tracking sheet, not in this repo (public repository).
- The anonymised rollup (§6) may be cited in the deck appendix as "n = 10 evaluator interviews, Sep–Oct 2026".

## 4. Script — 8 questions (ask in order; probes in italics)

1. **Walk me through how your last application round / deal was screened:** who read what, how many decks, how many minutes per deck? *Probe: how many people, how many stages, where does the first cut happen, what fraction gets a reply?*
2. **Which tools or subscriptions does the committee pay for today** (Dealum, F6S, Gust, Crunchbase, Techboard, spreadsheets) **and roughly what does each cost per year?** *Probe: who owns the login, what is a spreadsheet doing that a tool should, what was tried and dropped?*
3. **What does a "good" first-pass screen produce for you** — a rank, a shortlist, a red-flag list, a memo? **Who consumes it?** *Probe: what format lands in the committee pack, how long before decision day, does anyone read the whole deck after that?*
4. **Where did the last screening go wrong** — something missed, a disagreement you could not resolve, time overrun? *Probe: what did it cost (time, a deal, a sponsor conversation), what would have caught it?*
5. **If every applicant were scored on one rubric with cited evidence and an auditor's flags, what would you do differently on decision day?** *Probe: would you still read every deck, would you change the interview list, what would you argue about instead?*
6. **What would make you distrust an AI-generated score — and what would make you trust it enough to put it in front of the committee or a sponsor?** *Probe: method published, evidence visible, who verified what, track record vs outcomes, who else uses it, what happens when it is wrong?*
7. **Who approves a A$2,500–5,000/yr line item for the program or group, and what would they need to see first?** *Probe: budget cycle, sponsor / university / partner sign-off, per-seat vs per-cohort, what killed the last tool purchase?*
8. **After selection or investment, how do you get progress updates today, and what do you have to report upward** (sponsor, university, LPs) **and when?** *Probe: format, cadence, who chases founders, what is missing from the last report you sent?*

Close: "Is there anyone else who screens startups the way you do that I should talk to?" and the quote-approval reminder.
