## Day 4 — Fri 18 Sep — data principle — segment: everyone (founders first)

"Where does my data go?" is the first question a founder should ask any AI tool, so here is our answer, in full, unchanged from the privacy policy:

Your data belongs to your startup. We store it so every report builds on your own evidence and the AI reasons on your case. Founder-consented access tiers control who sees what.

What that means in practice on BlockID.au:

— A startup's evidence vault, score history and data room are its own. They accumulate so the next report starts from the record, not a blank page.
— If an investor or program adds your startup to evaluate it, you get a claim link. Accept it and you see what they see — and the consent tier decides what they can see of yours.
— Nothing about one startup is ever shown to another.

We put the same sentence on every evaluator page and on the comparison page, because a data policy that lives only in the footer isn't a policy.

Policy in full: https://blockid.au/legal/privacy?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=t1
