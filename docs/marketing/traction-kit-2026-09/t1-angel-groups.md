## 5. One-page FAQ (attach or paste under the signature)

**Why not just ask ChatGPT?**
ChatGPT gives you an outside-in opinion on whatever you paste, and a different one tomorrow. BlockID runs an inside-out review from the startup's own evidence — the same 8-dimension, 13-criteria rubric for every company, a full C-suite of specialist agents with Australian law and market context, an auditor that flags unsupported claims, and a score that updates every week as the company changes. You don't write forty prompts and stitch the answers together; you add the startup and read the report. Then you watch it move.

Full nine-row comparison (rubric, reviewers, evidence, updates, Australian context, confidentiality, audit trail, time, price): https://blockid.au/compare/chatgpt

**What data do you keep, and who sees it?**
Your data belongs to your startup. We store it so every report builds on your own evidence and the AI reasons on your case. Founder-consented access tiers control who sees what.
In practice: a startup you enter sits in your workspace; when you send the founder their claim link and they accept, the consent tier upgrades to "reports shared" and they can see and add to their own evidence. Nothing is shared between two evaluators unless one of them shares it. Privacy policy: https://blockid.au/legal/privacy

**What's in the A$3 report?**
A full Trust BizReport on a startup you enter: the 8-dimension, 13-criteria score with its reasoning, an AUD valuation range with the methods shown (Berkus, VC method, DCF, comparables against a curated set of Australian raises), the C-suite review by 11 C-Level agents, the auditor's flags, and a next-step plan, as a PDF. No subscription needed, and an ATO tax invoice on the charge. A re-score of a startup you already hold is A$1.

**What does the trial include, and what happens on day 8?**
The Scout workspace for 7 days: add the startups you're evaluating, run reports on the shared rubric, deal-flow browse, a watchlist, and the weekly Progress Radar. Card required at signup; nothing is billed if you cancel before day 7. On day 8 Scout starts at A$79 a month (10 reports a month included, 25 tracked startups). Firm (A$149, 3 seats, white-label PDF) and Program (A$349, 5 seats, batch scoring, sponsor/LP report) are the other rungs.

**Is a score a substitute for diligence?**
No. It is a triage layer built from what the founder gave us and what is publicly available, audited for unsupported claims. Nothing on the platform is financial or investment advice, and every decision remains yours.

**Where does the method come from?**
The scoring framework is documented in the public 12-chapter guide (https://blockid.au/guide) and grounded in the founder's doctoral research (DBA) on startup valuation.
