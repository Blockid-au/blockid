## 4. Ten anticipated questions and the replies

Reply in the founder's voice, under 120 words each, with a link where one exists. Never invent numbers.

1. **"Why not just ask ChatGPT?"**
   ChatGPT gives you an outside-in opinion on whatever you paste, and a different one tomorrow. BlockID runs an inside-out review from the startup's own evidence — the same 8-dimension, 13-criteria rubric for every company, a full C-suite of specialist agents with Australian law and market context, an auditor that flags unsupported claims, and a score that updates every week as the company changes. You don't write forty prompts and stitch the answers together; you add the startup and read the report. Then you watch it move. Honest nine-row comparison, including what ChatGPT is better at: blockid.au/compare/chatgpt

2. **"How is the score calculated?"**
   Eight dimensions, thirteen criteria, twelve growth phases; each judgement carries its reasoning so you can disagree with one line rather than the whole number. Eleven C-Level agents each own a domain module (CFO valuation, CLO legal incl. ESIC and s708, CMO market, CRO funding readiness, CTO, CHRO…), then an auditor checks the claims. The whole method is public: blockid.au/guide. It is grounded in the founder's doctoral research (DBA) on startup valuation.

3. **"What happens to my data / do you train on it?"**
   Your data belongs to your startup. We store it so every report builds on your own evidence and the AI reasons on your case. Founder-consented access tiers control who sees what. Policy: blockid.au/legal/privacy. (Do not add anything about training, in either direction.)

4. **"Is this a real valuation?"**
   No. It's a valuation *range* by Berkus, VC method, DCF and comparables against curated Australian raises — a screening and tracking instrument. For a transaction, a dispute or a tax matter you still need an independent valuer, and the report says so. When you need which: blockid.au/compare/valuers

5. **"What does it cost?"**
   First run free. A full Trust BizReport is A$3. Founders: Starter A$29/mo or Growth A$69/mo. Investors, advisors and programs: Scout A$79, Firm A$149, Program A$349 a month, each with a 7-day trial (card required, nothing billed if you cancel before day 7). All inc GST with an ATO tax invoice. blockid.au/pricing

6. **"Only Australia?"**
   Australian first: valuations in AUD, ESIC / R&D Tax Incentive / s708 checks, Australian grants and programs. The rubric itself isn't country-specific and anyone can run it; the grant directory and the legal checks are where the Australian depth is. Other markets when there's demand — tell me which.

7. **"How accurate is it?"**
   I won't quote an accuracy number I can't back. What I can say: the same rubric is applied to every company, every claim is tied to evidence or marked missing, and an auditor flags what isn't supported. `[[placeholder — collect from pilot: a program's own written read on committee agreement, if approved]]`

8. **"Which AI models do you use?"**
   A chain of providers, not one — the provider list is in the privacy policy and is kept in sync with what actually runs. Each agent has its own domain module and Australian context on top; the model is the engine, the rubric and the evidence are the product.

9. **"Can an investor see my startup without my permission?"**
   No. If an evaluator adds your startup, you get a claim link; accepting it upgrades the consent tier and you see what they see. Nothing about one startup is shown to another. Founder-consented access tiers control who sees what.

10. **"How is this different from a deck grader / a CRM / Crunchbase?"**
    Deck graders score decks; we score the company, and re-score it weekly as its evidence grows. CRMs track relationships and have no rubric. Data platforms tell you what happened; we tell you what's ready. And it's A$3, not a seat licence.
