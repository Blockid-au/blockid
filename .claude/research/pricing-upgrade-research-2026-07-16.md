# BlockID.au Pricing & Positioning Upgrade — Research Brief

**Date:** 2026-07-16
**Author:** Research (Claude)
**Scope:** Trial mechanics, competitor pricing, AU regulatory guardrails, equity-for-services norms, recommended tier structure.
**Status:** Advisory. Legal & tax items marked **[verify with counsel]** — none are legal advice.

## 1. Executive Summary (5 bullets)

- **Move to a 7-day card-required opt-out trial.** 2025-26 SaaS data shows opt-out (credit-card-required) trials convert at **31–49% trial→paid** vs 8–18% for opt-in; 7-day trials outperform longer ones (≈40% trial→paid median). BlockID's current "free forever + credits" model over-serves tyre-kickers and under-monetises intent.
- **Reposition around three buying jobs, not one product.** BlockID must sell three distinct SKUs at three price points: (i) Founder — a Carta-alternative + AI copilot at $29–$99/mo; (ii) Investor/Advisor — a portfolio + diligence lens at $79–$349/seat/mo; (iii) Accelerator/VC — a cohort + fund tier at $500–$2,500/mo + platform fee. Direct competitors (Carta, Pulley, Cake, Visible, Foundersuite, Crunchbase Pro) each own one slice — BlockID's edge is bundling them with SVI as the connective tissue.
- **Anchor on Cake Equity as the AU price ceiling.** Cake Equity (AU incumbent) charges A$1,000/yr entry, A$2,750/yr Team, custom Pro. BlockID's Founder tier must **undercut Cake by 30–50%** while offering AI copilot + SVI as differentiation — target A$49/mo (~A$588/yr) for Starter, A$149/mo for Growth.
- **Equity-for-services is a real wedge but a licensing minefield.** FAST/advisory-SAFE norms are well established (0.1–1.0% per advisor, 2yr monthly vest, no cliff or 3-mo cliff). BlockID can be the **workflow layer** (issue, track, vest, notify) but must not touch offer/facilitation of securities without ASIC review of RG 261/262/175 and (potentially) AFSL. **[verify with counsel]**
- **Instrument pre-seed metrics investors expect NOW.** Antler AU now writes $225K at higher valuations; Blackbird/Airtree/Startmate all look for **≥ A$1K MRR + 100+ users + one paid channel with CAC payback**. BlockID's current 0 MRR / 28 users is the gap — pricing changes below are designed to accelerate paid signal within 60 days.

## 2. Trial + Billing Best Practices (with numbers)

| Mechanic | 2025–26 benchmark | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Opt-out trial (CC at signup) trial→paid** | 31.4–48.8% median; elite 50–60% | Growthspree / 1Capture |
| **Opt-in trial (no CC) trial→paid** | 8.9–18.2% median | Growthspree / OpenView |
| **7-day trial conversion** | ~40.4% (highest of all trial lengths) | Growthspree |
| **60-day trial conversion** | ~30.6% (declines with length) | Growthspree |
| **Freemium → paid** | ~2.6% organic | ChartMogul |
| **Reverse trial (start paid features, downgrade later)** | 7–21% (+10–40% relative lift vs freemium) | OpenView / Zibly |
| **Trial-ending reminder timing** | Best practice: T-3d + T-1d + T+0h SMS/email; add "extend trial" CTA on cancel to recover ~5–10% | Industry norm |

**Recommendation for BlockID:**

1. **Primary flow: 7-day free trial, card required (opt-out), auto-charges A$29–$149/mo based on plan chosen.**
2. Keep **1 free SVI analysis** as an anonymous "taste" (like ahrefs' free traffic checker) — no signup required — to feed SEO/PLG top-of-funnel.
3. Kill the "Free forever + 2 credits" tier — cannibalising the paid entry. Replace with the 1 free anon SVI + card-gated trial.
4. Founding 100 (A$5 lifetime) stays as a scarcity/legacy hook until Stripe cap of 100 units hit, then retire.
5. Nurture: Day 0 (welcome + first analysis), Day 3 (evidence gap email), Day 5 (case study), Day 6 (trial-ending + upsell), Day 7 (charged/confirm), Day 14 (COMEBACK30 to cancels).

**Estimated funnel impact:** If BlockID drives 500 trial starts/month at 30% opt-out conversion → 150 paid × A$49 blended ARPU = **A$7,350 MRR by month 4**. Same 500 starts at current opt-in freemium (est. 3–5% conversion) → 15–25 paid = A$1,225 MRR. **5–6× revenue delta from mechanic change alone**, before pricing changes.

## 4. AU Regulatory Guardrails **[verify with counsel]**

- **Crowd-sourced funding (CSF):** ASIC RG 261 (companies) and RG 262 (intermediaries) — a company facilitating CSF offers is likely an intermediary and needs an AFSL with a CSF authorisation. **BlockID must NOT operate as a CSF intermediary without a licence.** Discovery / matching that stops short of facilitating an offer is currently the safer perimeter, but the line is a legal judgement.
- **Financial product advice (AFSL, Corps Act s911A):** Any output that a reasonable person could take as intended to influence a financial-product decision (e.g. "invest in this startup," "this valuation supports a $X investment") risks being financial-product advice and triggers AFSL requirements. BlockID's SVI + valuation must ship with the **general-advice-warning + not-a-formal-valuation disclaimer** on every report/PDF/API response. Do not personalise for individual investors' circumstances.
- **Managed investment schemes (MIS):** Pooling investor money via BlockID is out of perimeter without an MIS registration + Responsible Entity. Investor-facing tiers must be non-pooled (discovery/report only).
- **Digital shares / security tokens:** Under the Corporations Act, tokenised shares are still shares — same disclosure/prospectus rules apply. BlockID's on-chain layer must be **off-chain-first, blockchain-mirror-only**, with ASIC share register as source of truth (matches user's platform_vesting_blockchain memory).
- **Advisory-SAFE / equity-for-services:** Not itself a licensed activity, but issuing shares/options must follow s708 (small offer exemption) or an appropriate prospectus/exemption. Employee/contractor grants use ESS rules; advisor grants use s708 or director exemption. **[verify with counsel per issuer]**
- **GST on B2B SaaS:** AU-registered supplier — 10% GST applies once turnover ≥ A$75K in any rolling 12-month period. Business customers with an ABN can claim it back; consumer/prosumer cannot. Non-AU business customers may be zero-rated (export of services) if all recipient tests met.
- **Privacy Act 1988:** Handling cap-table + shareholder data triggers APP obligations; NDB scheme applies. Add data-breach notification plan before scaling investor tier.
- **Fair Trading / ACL:** Pricing displays must be all-inclusive of GST for consumer-facing pages. "Save X%" claims must be substantiated. Auto-renewal disclosures required at signup + at least once during trial (per ACCC guidance).

## 5. Equity-for-Services Norms (advisor, contractor)

| Stage | Typical advisor equity | Instrument | Vesting |
|---|---|---|---|
| Idea / pre-seed | 0.25–1.0% per advisor | FAST advisory agreement + option grant OR advisory SAFE | 2 years monthly, no cliff or 3-mo cliff |
| Seed | 0.15–0.5% | FAST + option grant | 2 years monthly, 3–6 mo cliff |
| Series A | 0.10–0.25% | FAST + option grant | 2 years monthly, 6-mo cliff |
| Series B+ | 0.05–0.15% | Options or warrants | 2 years monthly, 6-mo cliff, milestone gates |

- **Carta 2024 data:** median pre-seed advisor grant = **0.21%** of fully diluted shares.
- **FAST (Founder/Advisor Standard Template):** Founder Institute standard; v3 released June 2026. Defines equity level (Standard/Strategic/Expert × idea/startup/growth) mapping to 0.25%–1.0%.
- **Common instruments:** (a) FAST + NSO / unlisted option; (b) Warrant with fixed strike; (c) Advisory-SAFE (rare, mainly US); (d) Slicing Pie for pre-funding co-founder-like advisory.
- **Total advisor pool cap:** stay under 5% aggregate — larger pools flag governance issue to institutional investors.
- **Pitfalls:** dilution creep (no cap on advisor count), tax on option exercise (AU ESS rules), advisor churn without vesting cliff = permanent dead equity, misaligned advisors gaming the score.
- **BlockID product implication:** the "Equity-for-Services" workflow should default to FAST v3 terms, 2yr monthly vest, 0-cliff, and issue as a **notional grant record** on-chain-mirrored but ASIC-off-chain until legally executed.

- Accelerator tier prices track AcceleratorApp mid-market; F6S is free but hollow — BlockID sells outcome-tracking on top.
