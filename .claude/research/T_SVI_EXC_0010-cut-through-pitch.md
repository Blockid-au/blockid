# T_SVI_EXC_0010 — Cut Through Venture partnership pitch

**Owner:** IR · **Collaborators:** CMO, CRO
**Goal:** Cut Through Venture (CTV) embeds the BSI-AU widget in its weekly newsletter landing + about page, in exchange for co-branding of a monthly "BSI Movers" segment sourced from BlockID data.

## Why CTV
- Largest weekly AU/NZ startup funding newsletter (~50k subs, founder + investor heavy).
- Already publish a quarterly funding report — natural fit for an embedded live index.
- Founders Chris Gillings + Alexandra Solovetsky control editorial; small team = fast yes/no.

## Value exchange
| BlockID gives | CTV gives |
| --- | --- |
| Embeddable BSI-AU widget (free, attribution required) | Newsletter mention + landing page placement |
| Co-branded monthly "BSI movers" data drop (top 5 SVI gainers + sector flips) | Editorial endorsement / quote |
| Per-ticker widgets for any deal they cover | Hyperlink to startupvalueindex.com/listings |
| Quarterly Cut Through × BSI report co-authored | Distribution to their list |

## Success metric
- Tier 1: BSI-AU widget on CTV landing page + 1 newsletter mention by 2026-07-31.
- Tier 2: Monthly "BSI movers" segment by 2026-08-31.
- Tier 3: Quarterly co-authored BSI × Cut Through report (paid sponsorship slot exchange).

## Risks
- CTV monetises ad slots on the newsletter — they may push for a paid placement instead of editorial swap. Counter-offer: revenue share on premium institutional API tier referrals (T_0014).
- Editorial independence concerns if BlockID data drives "what to cover" decisions. Mitigation: data-drop is post-hoc, never feeds story selection.
