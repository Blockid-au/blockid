# T_SVI_EXC_0015 — Quarterly Cut Through × BSI Report · template

**Owner:** CMO · **Collaborators:** CDO, IR
**Goal:** Lock the editorial structure for the quarterly BSI-AU × Cut Through Venture market report. Co-branded distribution to ~50k AU/NZ investors + founders.

## Structure (every issue)

### 1. Headline metric box
- BSI-AU index level + Q-on-Q delta + drivers
- Total coverage A$
- Companies tracked
- New listings this quarter

### 2. Sector winners & losers
- Top 3 sectors by Δ Sector SVI
- Bottom 3 sectors
- 2-line narrative each (deal flow / down-rounds / regulatory)

### 3. Top movers (anonymous-by-default)
- Top 5 SVI gainers in the quarter
- Top 5 SVI losers
- Public name shown only if founder has opted in

### 4. Stage cohort
- New ideas (S1) tracked
- Series A graduations
- Reverse stages (down-rounded / closed)

### 5. Cap-table snapshot
- Total ESOP minted (A$)
- Average dilution per round (S2 → S3)
- Median runway

### 6. Themed deep-dive (rotating)
- Q3 26: AI Wrapper Survival Rate
- Q4 26: Climate-tech non-dilutive funding
- Q1 27: Healthtech reimbursement velocity
- Q2 27: Founder Profile completeness vs round-close speed

### 7. Cut Through commentary
- 300-word editorial from Cut Through editorial team
- Independent of BSI data — their reading of the quarter

### 8. Methodology + citation
- Cite as: *BlockID Startup Value Index™ × Cut Through Venture · Q[X] 20YY · startupvalueindex.com/reports*
- Methodology delta from previous quarter
- Data cutoff date

## Distribution
- PDF + microsite landing at startupvalueindex.com/reports/[quarter]
- Email blast — Cut Through list + BlockID list
- Press push: AFR, SmartCompany, Forbes AU
- LinkedIn carousel for the headline chart

## Production cadence
- T-30d: data cutoff
- T-21d: top movers + sector deltas computed
- T-14d: editorial draft + Cut Through commentary request
- T-7d: design layout + microsite QA
- T-0: publish + distribute
- T+7d: post-mortem (open rates, embeds, press pickup)

## Success metric
- ≥10k report PDF downloads or microsite visits per quarter
- ≥3 tier-1 press pickups (AFR/SmartCompany/Capital Brief)
- ≥1 institutional API tier (T_0014) signed in the quarter following each report
