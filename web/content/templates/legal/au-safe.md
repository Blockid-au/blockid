## 1. Events

### 1.1 Equity Financing
If there is an **Equity Financing** before the termination of this SAFE, on the closing of the Equity Financing the Company will automatically issue to the Investor a number of shares of **SAFE Preferred Stock** (defined below) equal to the Purchase Amount divided by the **Conversion Price**.

In connection with the automatic issuance of SAFE Preferred Stock under this section 1.1:
1. the Investor will execute and deliver the transaction documents required to be executed by other investors purchasing shares in the Equity Financing, provided that (a) the Investor's representations and warranties are limited to those given by the other investors, and (b) the Investor's economic terms are no less favourable than those given to the other investors;
2. the Company will issue SAFE Preferred Stock on terms that reflect the aggregate rights, preferences, and privileges of the standard preferred shares issued in the Equity Financing, but with a per-share liquidation preference equal to the Conversion Price and with anti-dilution and dividend rights adjusted accordingly (the **Conforming Provisions**); and
3. the issuance is subject to compliance with the pre-emptive rights (if any) in the Company's constitution and any Shareholders' Agreement.

### 1.2 Liquidity Event
If there is a **Liquidity Event** before the termination of this SAFE and before an Equity Financing, the Investor will, at the Investor's option, either:
1. receive a cash payment equal to the Purchase Amount (the **Cash-Out Amount**); or
2. automatically receive from the Company a number of Ordinary Shares equal to the Purchase Amount divided by the **Liquidity Price** (the **Conversion Amount**).

If the Investor fails to notify the Company of its election in writing within 5 Business Days after receiving notice of the Liquidity Event, the Investor is deemed to have elected the Conversion Amount.

If, in a Change of Control, the acquiring party requires that any Cash-Out Amount payable to Investor be paid on the same terms as the Ordinary Shareholders, then the Cash-Out Amount will be paid pro rata with the payments to Ordinary Shareholders and, if necessary, on the same schedule.

### 1.3 Dissolution Event
If there is a **Dissolution Event** before this SAFE has terminated in accordance with section 1.1 or section 1.2, the Company will pay to the Investor an amount equal to the Purchase Amount, due and payable to the Investor immediately before the completion of the Dissolution Event, subject to the Corporations Act (including s254T solvency test) and to the priority claims of creditors and any preferred securityholders.

### 1.4 Termination
This SAFE terminates (without any further liability of either party) on the earliest of:
1. the issuance of shares to the Investor under section 1.1 or section 1.2;
2. the payment (or setting aside for payment) of amounts due to the Investor under section 1.2 or section 1.3.

## 2. Definitions

**Business Day** means a day (other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday) on which trading banks are open for business in Sydney, New South Wales.

**Change of Control** means (i) a transaction or series of related transactions in which any person or entity (together with associates as defined in s11 of the Corporations Act) becomes the beneficial owner, directly or indirectly, of more than 50% of the outstanding voting shares of the Company; (ii) any reorganisation, merger, consolidation, or scheme of arrangement resulting in the Shareholders of the Company immediately before the transaction ceasing to hold a majority of the voting equity of the surviving entity; or (iii) a sale, lease, exclusive licence, or other disposition of all or substantially all of the assets of the Company.

**Company Capitalisation** is calculated as of immediately before the Equity Financing and (without double-counting):
1. includes all shares of capital stock issued and outstanding;
2. includes all Converting Securities (as defined below), including this SAFE and all other SAFEs;
3. includes all Options and warrants that are outstanding;
4. includes the Unissued Option Pool;
but **excludes** the Shares of Standard Preferred Stock issued in the Equity Financing.

**Conversion Price** means either (as applicable):
- {{#variant_cap_only}} the **SAFE Price**; {{/variant_cap_only}}
- {{#variant_discount_only}} the **Discount Price**; {{/variant_discount_only}}
- {{#variant_cap_and_discount}} the lower of (a) the SAFE Price and (b) the Discount Price. {{/variant_cap_and_discount}}

**Converting Securities** includes this SAFE and any other convertible securities issued by the Company, including all other SAFEs and convertible notes, that convert or are convertible into equity in connection with an Equity Financing.

**Discount Price** means the price per share of Standard Preferred Stock sold in the Equity Financing multiplied by the Discount Rate.

**Discount Rate** means (100% minus **{{discount_rate}}%**) — for example, if {{discount_rate}} is 20, the Discount Rate is 80%.

**Dissolution Event** means (i) a voluntary termination of operations of the Company; (ii) a general assignment for the benefit of the Company's creditors; or (iii) any other liquidation, dissolution, or winding-up of the Company (but excluding a Liquidity Event or a solvent scheme of arrangement).

**Dividend Amount** means, with respect to any date on which the Company pays a dividend on its outstanding Ordinary Shares, the amount of such dividend that is paid per Ordinary Share multiplied by (x) the Purchase Amount divided by (y) the Liquidity Price.

**Equity Financing** means a bona fide transaction or series of transactions with the principal purpose of raising capital, pursuant to which the Company issues and sells Preferred Stock at a fixed pre-money valuation to bona fide investors.

**Initial Public Offering** means the closing of the Company's first firm-commitment underwritten initial public offering of Ordinary Shares under a prospectus lodged with ASIC (or an equivalent regulator in another jurisdiction) leading to admission to the official list of the Australian Securities Exchange or another recognised stock exchange.

**Liquidity Capitalisation** is calculated as of immediately before the Liquidity Event, and (without double-counting):
1. includes all shares of capital stock issued and outstanding;
2. includes all Converting Securities (other than this SAFE);
3. includes all Options and warrants that are outstanding;
but **excludes**: (a) the Unissued Option Pool; and (b) any Shares reserved and available for future grant under an Employee Share Option Plan.

**Liquidity Event** means a Change of Control or an Initial Public Offering.

**Liquidity Price** means the price per share equal to the Post-Money Valuation Cap divided by the Liquidity Capitalisation.

**Options** includes options, restricted share awards or purchases, rights, warrants, or similar rights to purchase or acquire Ordinary Shares of the Company.

**Ordinary Shares** means fully paid ordinary shares in the capital of the Company.

**Post-Money Valuation Cap** means **A${{valuation_cap_aud}}**.

**Preferred Stock** or **Preference Shares** means preference shares of the Company issued in an Equity Financing on the terms recorded in the transaction documents for that Equity Financing.

**Pro Rata Rights** means the rights (if granted separately in a Pro Rata Side Letter — see clause 8.6) allowing the Investor to participate in future Equity Financings up to its Pro Rata Percentage.

**Pro Rata Percentage** means, with respect to the Investor and any Equity Financing after the Equity Financing in which this SAFE converts, the ratio (expressed as a percentage) of (x) the number of shares held by the Investor upon conversion of this SAFE to (y) the total Company Capitalisation immediately before that later Equity Financing.

**SAFE Preferred Stock** means the shares of Preferred Stock issued to the Investor in an Equity Financing, having the same rights and preferences as the Standard Preferred Stock issued in the Equity Financing except that: (i) the per-share liquidation preference and initial conversion price will be equal to the Conversion Price; and (ii) the basis for any dividend or other rights denominated in dollar amounts is adjusted proportionally.

**SAFE Price** means the price per share equal to the Post-Money Valuation Cap divided by the Company Capitalisation.

**Shareholder Agreement** or **Shareholders' Agreement** means the agreement entered into in connection with an Equity Financing that regulates the rights and obligations of the Shareholders as a group.

**Standard Preferred Stock** means the shares of Preferred Stock issued to the new investors in an Equity Financing at the fixed price per share set for that Equity Financing.

**Unissued Option Pool** means all Shares (whether or not issued) that are reserved and available for future grant under any employee share option plan or similar plan of the Company (including the post-financing option pool required by the investors in an Equity Financing).

6. it is not a party to any litigation, arbitration, or regulatory proceeding likely to have a material adverse effect on its business, and no such proceeding is threatened against it.

6. it has obtained (or has had the opportunity to obtain) independent legal, tax, and financial advice about this SAFE.

## 5. Procedures

### 5.1 Notice of Equity Financing / Liquidity Event / Dissolution Event
The Company will give the Investor written notice of an anticipated Equity Financing, Liquidity Event, or Dissolution Event at least 5 Business Days before the completion of such event (or such shorter period as may be necessary given the timing of the event).

### 5.2 Corporations Act compliance
The issue of shares under section 1.1, section 1.2, or a Pro Rata Side Letter will be undertaken in compliance with (a) the pre-emptive rights provisions of the Company's constitution and any Shareholders' Agreement then in force; and (b) any applicable disclosure exemption under Chapter 6D of the Corporations Act.

### 5.3 No transfer without consent
The Investor may not transfer this SAFE (in whole or in part) without the prior written consent of the Company, other than to (a) an entity wholly owned by the Investor; (b) a family trust of the Investor; or (c) an affiliate of the Investor (each a **Permitted Transferee**). Any Permitted Transferee must first execute a deed of accession to this SAFE and confirm the Investor Representations in section 4.

## 6. Most-Favoured Nation

### 6.1 MFN
If the Company issues any Additional SAFE (as defined below) to any other investor after the date of this SAFE on terms more favourable, in the Investor's reasonable judgement, to that investor than the terms in this SAFE, then the Company will promptly provide the Investor with written notice of the Additional SAFE together with a copy of it, and the Investor may elect (by written notice within 20 Business Days) to amend and restate this SAFE to reflect the terms of the Additional SAFE in their entirety.

### 6.2 Additional SAFE
"**Additional SAFE**" means any Simple Agreement for Future Equity (or similar convertible instrument) issued by the Company after the date of this SAFE and before the earlier of an Equity Financing or a Liquidity Event, other than: (a) shares of Standard Preferred Stock issued in an Equity Financing; (b) options granted under an employee share option plan; and (c) shares issued on the exercise of options or the conversion of Converting Securities.

## 8. Miscellaneous

### 8.1 Amendment
Any provision of this SAFE may be amended, waived, or modified by written consent of the Company and either (i) the Investor or (ii) the majority-in-interest of all then-outstanding SAFEs with the same Post-Money Valuation Cap and matching MFN provisions, provided that (a) any such amendment must apply to all such SAFEs; and (b) no amendment may adversely affect the specific rights of any Investor without that Investor's consent.

### 8.2 Notices
Any notice under this SAFE must be in writing and delivered by hand, prepaid post, or email to the address recorded on the first page (or such other address as a party notifies to the other in writing). Notices are effective (i) if delivered by hand, on delivery; (ii) if posted, 3 Business Days after posting; (iii) if sent by email, when the sender's system logs the email as sent, provided no bounce-back is received within 4 hours.

### 8.3 Successors and Assigns
The rights and obligations under this SAFE are binding on and enforceable by the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

### 8.4 Currency
All amounts payable under this SAFE are in **Australian dollars (AUD)**.

### 8.5 Governing law and dispute resolution
This SAFE is governed by the laws of **{{governing_state}}** (default: New South Wales), Australia. The parties submit to the non-exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of {{governing_state}}. Any dispute must first be referred to mediation administered by the Australian Disputes Centre, and if unresolved within 30 days, may be referred to arbitration under the ACICA Arbitration Rules seated in Sydney. Nothing in this clause prevents a party from seeking urgent injunctive relief.

### 8.6 Pro-Rata Rights (side letter)
Pro-Rata Rights are **not** included in this SAFE by default. If the Investor wishes to obtain Pro-Rata Rights, the parties may enter into a separate Pro-Rata Side Letter substantially in the form maintained by BlockID.au (available on request), granting the Investor the right to participate in a future Equity Financing up to its Pro Rata Percentage on the same terms as the other new investors.

### 8.7 Severability
If any provision of this SAFE is held to be unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue in full force and effect.

### 8.8 Counterparts and electronic signatures
This SAFE may be executed in counterparts (including by electronic signature under s10 of the *Electronic Transactions Act 1999* (Cth) and equivalent State/Territory legislation), each of which constitutes an original.

### 8.9 Entire agreement
This SAFE, together with any Pro-Rata Side Letter, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties in relation to the subject matter, and supersedes all prior negotiations, understandings, and agreements.

### 8.10 GST
All amounts payable under this SAFE are exclusive of GST unless expressly stated to be inclusive. The parties do not consider any supply under this SAFE to be a taxable supply for the purposes of the *A New Tax System (Goods and Services Tax) Act 1999* (Cth), but each party will comply with its GST obligations if the ATO determines otherwise.

### 8.11 Stamp duty
The Company is responsible for any stamp duty payable in connection with this SAFE.
