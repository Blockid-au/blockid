## 5. Confidentiality (binding)

5.1 The parties acknowledge that they may exchange confidential
information in the course of progressing this LOI. Each party will:

- (a) use the other party's confidential information only for the purpose
      of evaluating a possible Definitive Agreement;
- (b) protect the other party's confidential information using at least
      the same standard of care it applies to its own confidential
      information (and in any event no less than reasonable care); and
- (c) not disclose the other party's confidential information to any
      third party without the disclosing party's prior written consent,
      except to its personnel, professional advisers, or contractors who
      are subject to obligations of confidence.

5.2 This paragraph 5 (Confidentiality) and paragraphs 8 (Non-binding
nature), 9 (Publicity), 10 (Costs), and 11 (Governing law and
jurisdiction) are intended to be legally binding on the parties. All
other paragraphs are non-binding statements of present intention only.

## 6. Non-binding nature of commercial terms

6.1 Save as expressly stated in paragraph 5.2, no legal or equitable
obligation arises from this LOI. In particular:

- (a) neither party is obliged to enter into a Definitive Agreement;
- (b) neither party is entitled to compensation for costs incurred in
      progressing the discussions contemplated by this LOI; and
- (c) either party may terminate the discussions at any time and for any
      reason by written notice to the other, without incurring any
      liability to the other party.

6.2 The Customer acknowledges that the Supplier may:

- (a) rely on this LOI as evidence of a genuine, arms-length indication of
      commercial demand for the Product for the purpose of a capital raise
      or fundraise data room; and
- (b) provided the Supplier discloses the non-binding nature of this LOI,
      the total indicative annual contract value, and the identity of the
      Customer (subject to paragraph 9 below), disclose this LOI to
      prospective investors, professional advisers, and lenders of the
      Supplier.

6.3 The Supplier will not:

- (a) describe this LOI as a binding purchase order, executed contract,
      or committed revenue in any communication to a third party;
- (b) publicly represent the amount of any indicative contract value
      without stating that it is indicative and non-binding; or
- (c) issue any statement that could be misleading or deceptive under
      s18 of the *Australian Consumer Law* (Schedule 2 to the
      *Competition and Consumer Act 2010* (Cth)) or under s1041H of the
      *Corporations Act 2001* (Cth) in relation to this LOI.

## 8. Non-binding nature (confirmation)

8.1 The parties confirm that they do not intend for this LOI, other than
paragraphs 5.2, 9, 10, and 11, to give rise to legal relations. If any
provision is nonetheless held to be binding, the parties agree it is to
be construed narrowly and in a manner consistent with the balance of this
LOI remaining an expression of intention only.

## 9. Publicity

9.1 Neither party will use the other party's name, logo, or brand
identifiers in any public communication (including on a public-facing
website, investor deck, press release, or media briefing) without the
other party's prior written consent.

9.2 Notwithstanding paragraph 9.1, the Supplier may:

- (a) disclose the Customer's name to prospective investors, professional
      advisers, lenders, or grant assessors under standard confidentiality
      obligations, provided the Supplier clearly states that this LOI is
      non-binding; and
- (b) refer to the Customer's industry sector and business size in
      aggregated, non-identifying form without further consent.

## 10. Costs

progressing this LOI and any subsequent Definitive Agreement, unless
otherwise agreed in writing.
