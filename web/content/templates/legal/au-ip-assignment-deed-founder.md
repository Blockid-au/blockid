## Recitals

A. The Founder has, in the period from the earliest date of any conceptualisation, sketch, code commit, design, or written material relating to the business of the Company through to the Effective Date, created or contributed to certain Intellectual Property Rights (as defined in section 2) directly relevant to the business of the Company (the **Assigned IP**).

B. The Company is engaged in the business described as: {{company_business_description}}.

C. The Parties intend that all Assigned IP vest exclusively in the Company from the Effective Date, and (to the extent permissible at law) with retrospective effect from the earliest date of creation.

D. This Deed is executed to give effect to that intention and to give investors, acquirers, and directors comfort that the Company owns 100% of the Intellectual Property Rights required to operate the business, in accordance with due-diligence standards commonly applied in Australian venture capital transactions.

## 1. Assignment of Intellectual Property

### 1.1 Present assignment
The Founder, with effect from the Effective Date, assigns to the Company absolutely all right, title, and interest (including all Intellectual Property Rights, whether registered or unregistered, existing anywhere in the world, present and future) in and to the Assigned IP, including without limitation:

1. all copyright subsisting in any works created by the Founder (whether alone or jointly) that relate to the business of the Company under the **Copyright Act 1968** (Cth);
2. all rights in any invention, discovery, or improvement (whether patentable or not) under the **Patents Act 1990** (Cth), including the exclusive right to apply for patent protection in any jurisdiction and to claim priority from any earlier filing;
3. all rights in registered and unregistered designs under the **Designs Act 2003** (Cth);
4. all rights in trade marks (registered and unregistered), business names, domain names, and get-up under the **Trade Marks Act 1995** (Cth) and at common law;
5. all rights in circuit layouts under the **Circuit Layouts Act 1989** (Cth);
6. all rights in plant breeder's rights (if any) under the **Plant Breeder's Rights Act 1994** (Cth);
7. all rights in confidential information, trade secrets, know-how, methodologies, processes, and data compilations;
8. all rights in databases and data (whether protected by copyright as a compilation or otherwise); and
9. all rights of a similar nature to the foregoing, anywhere in the world.

### 1.2 Retrospective effect
To the fullest extent permissible at law, the assignment in section 1.1 has effect from the earliest date on which any element of the Assigned IP was first created or reduced to material form (the **Creation Date**). The Founder holds all such rights on trust for the Company from the Creation Date to the Effective Date and any beneficial interest in the Assigned IP vests in the Company on the Effective Date.

### 1.3 Future IP
The Founder agrees that any Intellectual Property Rights created, developed, or acquired by the Founder in the course of, or in connection with, the Founder's role with the Company (whether as founder, director, employee, consultant, or otherwise) after the Effective Date automatically vest in the Company on creation, without further act. If any such assignment is not effective at law, the Founder holds those rights on trust for the Company pending assignment.

## 2. Definitions

**Assigned IP** has the meaning given in the recitals, but excludes the Excluded IP.

**Intellectual Property Rights** means all industrial and intellectual property rights of any kind, whether registered or unregistered, arising anywhere in the world, including without limitation the rights described in section 1.1.

**Excluded IP** has the meaning given in section 1.4.

## 3. Moral rights consent

### 3.1 Consent
The Founder is the **author** (within the meaning of Part IX of the **Copyright Act 1968** (Cth)) of certain works comprising the Assigned IP. The Founder consents, unconditionally and irrevocably, under section 195AW and section 195AWA of the Copyright Act to all acts or omissions by the Company (and its licensees, successors, and assigns) that would, but for this consent, infringe any of the Founder's moral rights (as defined in section 189 of the Copyright Act) in any existing or future works.

### 3.2 Scope
The consent given under section 3.1 extends to:
1. the right of **attribution** of authorship (section 193);
2. the right **not to have authorship falsely attributed** (section 195AC); and
3. the right of **integrity of authorship** (section 195AI),

in respect of any and all uses, modifications, adaptations, translations, derivative works, or dispositions of the Assigned IP.

### 3.3 No performers' rights waiver
Nothing in this Deed operates to waive any moral rights that cannot be consented to or waived under Australian law.

## 4. Warranties

### 4.1 Founder warranties
The Founder warrants to the Company, as at the Effective Date and continuing, that:

1. the Founder is the sole legal and beneficial owner of the Assigned IP and has full authority to assign it;
2. no part of the Assigned IP infringes the rights of any third party (including any employer, university, or prior client), and no third party has any claim, lien, encumbrance, licence, or right of pre-emption over the Assigned IP;
3. the Founder has not previously assigned, licensed, or purported to assign or license any of the Assigned IP to any other person;
4. the Founder has not received any consideration (grant funding, prize money, or otherwise) that requires the Assigned IP to be licensed to any third party (including a university, research institution, or CRC), except as disclosed in Annexure A; and
5. the Assigned IP does not include any material subject to a Creative Commons, GPL, or other open-source licence that would encumber the Company's use of the Assigned IP for its business, except as disclosed in Annexure A.

### 4.2 Prior-employer / university IP
The Founder specifically confirms that the Assigned IP was not created:
1. during work hours or using resources of any prior employer whose employment contract required IP assignment to that employer; or
2. as part of research at any university or publicly funded research institution whose IP policy would vest ownership in that institution,

except as expressly disclosed in Annexure A. The Founder acknowledges that a breach of this warranty could result in the Company losing the right to exploit the Assigned IP and may give rise to third-party claims.

### 4.3 Survival
The warranties in this section 4 survive execution of this Deed and any subsequent sale, financing, or reorganisation of the Company.

## 5. Further assurances

### 5.1 Registration and perfection
The Founder must, at the Company's cost, promptly do all things and execute all documents (including any assignment, deed of confirmation, patent-application transfer, or trade-mark assignment form) that the Company reasonably requests to:
1. vest the Assigned IP in the Company;
2. register the Company as the owner of any registered or applied-for Intellectual Property Rights within the Assigned IP; or
3. record the assignment on any register maintained by IP Australia, USPTO, EPO, or any other IP office.

### 5.2 Power of attorney
The Founder irrevocably appoints the Company (and each of its directors from time to time) as the Founder's attorney to execute in the Founder's name any document the Company considers necessary to give effect to section 5.1. The Founder ratifies and confirms anything the attorney does under this power. This power is granted for valuable consideration (namely the Company's ongoing engagement of the Founder) and is granted by way of security.

### 5.3 PPSR
Where any part of the Assigned IP constitutes personal property under the **Personal Property Securities Act 2009** (Cth), the Founder consents to the Company registering the assignment on the Personal Property Securities Register.

## 6. Restrictive covenants

### 6.1 Confidentiality
The Founder must not, at any time after the Effective Date, disclose or use any confidential information forming part of the Assigned IP other than for the benefit of the Company or with the Company's prior written consent.

### 6.2 Non-compete (post-departure)
For a period of {{restraint_period_months}} months after the Founder ceases to hold any role with the Company, the Founder must not, within the geographical area of {{restraint_geography}}, directly or indirectly engage in any business that competes with the Company's business as at the date of departure. This restraint is drafted for severance under the **Restraints of Trade Act 1976** (NSW) — a court may sever any portion held to be unreasonable and enforce the remainder. This clause applies only to the extent of {{restraint_scope}}.

## 7. General

### 7.1 Governing law
This Deed is governed by the laws of {{governing_state}}, Australia. Each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of that State and the Federal Court of Australia sitting in that State.

### 7.2 Entire agreement
This Deed contains the entire agreement between the Parties as to its subject matter and supersedes all prior agreements, arrangements, and understandings.

### 7.3 Variation
No variation is effective unless it is in writing and signed by both Parties (or executed as a deed if required).

### 7.4 Severability
If any clause of this Deed is held invalid or unenforceable, that clause is severed to the minimum extent necessary and the balance of this Deed continues in full force.

### 7.5 Counterparts
This Deed may be executed in any number of counterparts, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one and the same instrument.

### 7.6 Electronic execution
The Parties agree that this Deed may be signed by electronic signature and consent to the use of electronic signatures under the **Electronic Transactions Act 1999** (Cth) (and equivalent State/Territory legislation, including in respect of a document taking effect as a deed). Each Party agrees that (a) an electronic signature affixed by that Party or with that Party's authority binds that Party as if it were a manuscript signature; (b) a scanned image, PDF, or e-signature platform (such as DocuSign, SignNow, or Adobe Sign) constitutes sufficient execution; and (c) the requirement (if any) at general law for a deed to be signed, sealed, and delivered is satisfied by the Parties' electronic signatures and this express agreement.

### 7.7 Deed
This document is executed as a deed. The Parties intend it to take effect as a deed and not merely as a contract, notwithstanding that consideration may not have passed between them.
