# {{scheme_name}} — Employee Share Option Plan Rules

**Company:** {{company_name}} Pty Ltd (ACN {{acn}}) (the **Company**)
**Scheme Name:** {{scheme_name}} (the **Plan**)
**Adoption Date:** {{adoption_date}}
**Version:** 1.0 (BlockID.au AU ESOP Scheme Rules — Round 5.6b)

These Plan Rules are adopted by resolution of the Board of the Company on {{adoption_date}} and, where required, by the Shareholders of the Company. They govern the grant, vesting, exercise, and lapsing of Options issued under the Plan to eligible Participants.

---

## 1. Purpose of the Plan

### 1.1 Purpose
The purpose of the Plan is to:
1. attract, retain, and motivate high-performing employees, executive directors, and contractors of the Company and its subsidiaries;
2. align the economic interests of Participants with the interests of Shareholders in the long-term growth of the Company; and
3. where the eligibility criteria are met, deliver equity to Participants on terms that qualify for the **start-up concession** in Subdivision 83A-B and the concessional treatment in Subdivision 83A-C of the ITAA97.

### 1.2 Compliance framework
The Plan is intended to operate in compliance with:
- Division 83A of the ITAA97 (employee share schemes);
- Chapter 6D and Chapter 7 of the *Corporations Act 2001* (Cth), including the disclosure relief in ASIC Class Order [CO 14/1000] / ASIC Instrument 2022/1021 (or successor instruments);
- the *Fair Work Act 2009* (Cth); and
- the tax reporting obligations imposed on employers under s392-5 of the *Taxation Administration Act 1953* (Cth) (annual ESS statement).

## 2. Definitions

In these Rules, unless the context requires otherwise:

- **Applicable Law** means all statutes, regulations, ASIC instruments, ATO rulings, and stock exchange rules that apply to the Plan or to Participants.
- **Bad Leaver** means a Participant whose engagement with the Company or a Group Member ceases in circumstances of: (a) summary dismissal for serious misconduct (as defined in the Fair Work Regulations 2009); (b) resignation before the Cliff; (c) material breach of an IP-assignment, confidentiality, non-compete, or non-solicit obligation to the Company; or (d) fraud, dishonesty, or a criminal conviction affecting the Company.
- **Board** means the board of directors of the Company or a committee to which the Board has delegated its powers under these Rules.
- **Change of Control** means the earlier of: (a) a person (together with their Associates as defined in s11 of the Corporations Act) acquiring more than 50% of the Voting Shares of the Company; (b) the sale of all or substantially all of the assets of the Company; or (c) a merger, scheme of arrangement, or similar transaction that results in the Shareholders of the Company immediately before the transaction ceasing to hold a majority of the voting equity of the surviving entity.
- **Cliff** means the initial period (default {{default_cliff_months}} months, being 12 months) during which no Options vest, as specified in the Grant Letter.
- **Company** means {{company_name}} Pty Ltd (ACN {{acn}}).
- **Div 83A Start-up Concession** means the tax concession available under Subdivision 83A-B where the conditions in ss83A-33 and 83A-45 of the ITAA97 are met (see clause 8).
- **Eligible Person** means a person eligible to participate in the Plan under clause 4.
- **ESS Interest** means an ESS interest as defined in s83A-10(1) of the ITAA97, being (in the case of the Plan) a beneficial interest in a right to acquire a beneficial interest in a Share.
- **Exercise Notice** means the notice served by a Participant to exercise Vested Options under clause 7.
- **Exercise Price** means the price payable per Share on exercise of an Option, as specified in the Grant Letter, which must be no less than the Fair Market Value of a Share on the Grant Date if the Grant is intended to satisfy s83A-33(3) of the ITAA97.
- **Expiry Date** means the date on which an Option lapses, being no later than **10 years** after the Grant Date (per s83A-33(1)(d) of the ITAA97 as it applies to start-up concession eligibility).
- **Fair Market Value** means, in respect of a Share, the value determined by the Board acting reasonably by reference to (i) the price of Shares in the most recent priced equity round; or (ii) an independent valuation obtained under one of the safe-harbour valuation methods in *Income Tax Assessment (1997 Act) Regulations 2021*, Regulation 83A-315.02 (net tangible assets method or unlisted market value method).
- **Good Leaver** means a Participant whose engagement with the Company or a Group Member ceases for any reason other than as a Bad Leaver, including resignation with Board consent after the Cliff, redundancy, retirement, death, or Total and Permanent Disability.
- **Grant Date** means the date on which the Board resolves to grant an Option to a Participant.
- **Grant Letter** means the letter of offer issued to a Participant recording the material terms of a Grant, in substantially the form in Schedule 1.
- **Group Member** means the Company or any subsidiary of the Company.
- **Option** means a right granted to a Participant under the Plan to acquire one Share upon exercise and payment of the Exercise Price.
- **Participant** means an Eligible Person who has been granted an Option and has accepted the Grant.
- **Plan** means the {{scheme_name}} governed by these Rules.
- **Reserve Pool** has the meaning in clause 3.1.
- **Rules** means these Employee Share Option Plan Rules, as amended from time to time.
- **Share** means a fully paid ordinary share in the capital of the Company.
- **Vesting Commencement Date** means the date from which vesting is calculated under the Grant Letter (typically the date the Participant commenced their engagement with a Group Member, or such earlier date as the Board approves).
- **Vested Option** means an Option in respect of which the applicable vesting conditions have been satisfied.

## 3. The Plan and the Reserve Pool

### 3.1 Reserve Pool
The Board is authorised to grant Options in respect of a maximum number of Shares equal to **{{reserve_pool_pct}}%** (default 10%) of the fully diluted share capital of the Company from time to time (the **Reserve Pool**). The Reserve Pool refreshes automatically on each priced equity financing to restore the pool to {{reserve_pool_pct}}% of post-money fully diluted capital, provided the refresh is approved by the Board and (where required by the constitution or any Shareholders' Agreement) by the Shareholders.

### 3.2 Increases to the Reserve Pool
Any increase to the Reserve Pool above the size referenced in clause 3.1 requires the approval of the Shareholders by ordinary resolution and, where necessary, by Special Resolution to displace pre-emptive rights.

### 3.3 Reissue of lapsed Options
Options that lapse, are forfeited, or are cancelled are returned to the Reserve Pool and may be re-granted, subject to Applicable Law.

## 4. Eligibility

### 4.1 Eligible Persons
The Board may invite any of the following to participate in the Plan:
1. an employee (permanent full-time, permanent part-time, or fixed-term) of a Group Member;
2. a full-time or part-time executive director of a Group Member;
3. an "eligible contractor" as defined in s1100Q of the Corporations Act, being a contractor who genuinely spends the equivalent of at least 40% of their working hours engaged by the Company; or
4. any other person the Board considers appropriate, provided the participation of that person does not jeopardise the availability of the Div 83A Start-up Concession or any Corporations Act disclosure exemption.

### 4.2 Div 83A eligibility filter
Where the Grant is intended to satisfy the Div 83A Start-up Concession, the Board must confirm before Grant that (a) the Participant is an employee of the Company or a subsidiary of the Company (per s83A-45(1)); and (b) the Participant, immediately after the Grant, will not hold a beneficial interest in more than 10% of the Shares of the Company or be able to control more than 10% of the voting rights (per s83A-45(6)).

### 4.3 Foreign Participants
The Board may impose additional conditions on Grants to Participants who are not Australian tax residents to address foreign securities laws, tax reporting, and withholding obligations.

## 5. Grant of Options

### 5.1 Board discretion
The Board may in its absolute discretion grant Options to any Eligible Person on such terms as it determines, subject to these Rules. No Eligible Person has a right to be granted Options.

### 5.2 Grant Letter
Each Grant must be evidenced by a Grant Letter substantially in the form in Schedule 1, setting out:
1. the Participant's name and role;
2. the Grant Date and the Vesting Commencement Date;
3. the number of Options granted;
4. the Exercise Price per Share;
5. the vesting schedule (Cliff, monthly, or milestone-based);
6. the Expiry Date (no later than 10 years after Grant);
7. whether the Grant is intended to qualify for the Div 83A Start-up Concession;
8. any performance conditions or milestones;
9. Change of Control treatment (single trigger or double trigger — clause 10); and
10. the applicable Good Leaver / Bad Leaver treatment (clause 9).

### 5.3 Acceptance
An Eligible Person accepts a Grant by signing and returning the Grant Letter within 30 days after the Grant Date, failing which the Board may withdraw the Grant. Acceptance constitutes agreement to be bound by these Rules.

### 5.4 No consideration payable on Grant
No consideration is payable by the Participant on Grant. Consideration (the Exercise Price) is payable only on exercise under clause 7.

## 6. Vesting

### 6.1 Default vesting schedule (time-based)
Unless the Grant Letter specifies otherwise, Options vest as follows:
1. **Cliff:** No Options vest until the {{default_cliff_months}}-month (default 12-month) anniversary of the Vesting Commencement Date. On that date, **25%** of the Options vest.
2. **Monthly vesting thereafter:** The remaining 75% of the Options vest in equal monthly instalments over the remaining **{{default_vest_months}}** months (default 36 months, so that the total vesting period is 48 months / 4 years).

### 6.2 Milestone-based vesting (variant)
Where the Grant Letter specifies milestone-based vesting, Options vest on the achievement of the milestones listed in the Grant Letter as determined by the Board acting reasonably. Where a milestone is only partially achieved, the Board may in its discretion vest a pro-rata number of Options.

### 6.3 Continuous engagement required
Except as provided in clauses 9 and 10, Options only vest while the Participant remains continuously engaged by a Group Member. Approved parental, personal, or long-service leave does not interrupt continuous engagement.

### 6.4 Real risk of forfeiture
The Board confirms that the vesting conditions in this clause 6 are intended to create a **real risk of forfeiture** for the purposes of s83A-33(1)(a) and s83A-105(6) of the ITAA97, so that a Participant does not, under a reasonable person test, be considered to have no real risk of losing the ESS Interest.

## 7. Exercise of Options

### 7.1 Exercise window
A Participant may exercise Vested Options at any time from the date of vesting until the earlier of:
1. the Expiry Date;
2. the date the Option lapses under clause 9 or clause 10; or
3. the date of a Change of Control (subject to clause 10).

### 7.2 Exercise Notice
To exercise Vested Options, a Participant must deliver to the Company an Exercise Notice specifying:
1. the number of Vested Options being exercised; and
2. payment of the aggregate Exercise Price (or an election to use the cashless exercise mechanism in clause 7.3).

### 7.3 Cashless exercise
Where the Board approves, a Participant may cashlessly exercise Vested Options by electing to receive that number of Shares equal to (a) the number of Options exercised multiplied by (b) the difference between the Fair Market Value of a Share and the Exercise Price, divided by (c) the Fair Market Value of a Share.

### 7.4 Issue of Shares
On receipt of a valid Exercise Notice and payment of the Exercise Price, the Company must, within 30 days:
1. issue the Shares to the Participant;
2. enter the Participant on the Register of Members;
3. deliver a holding statement or share certificate; and
4. procure that the Participant executes a deed of accession to any Shareholders' Agreement then in force.

### 7.5 Tax withholding
Where the Div 83A Start-up Concession does not apply to a Grant, the Company may withhold from any payment to the Participant such amount as it reasonably considers necessary to satisfy any PAYG withholding obligation triggered by the exercise (see s83A-25 of the ITAA97 and ss12-35 to 12-60 of Schedule 1 to the *Taxation Administration Act 1953* (Cth)).

## 8. Div 83A Start-up Concession Compliance

### 8.1 Company conditions (per s83A-45)
The Board acknowledges that the Div 83A Start-up Concession only applies where, at the time of the Grant:
1. no equity interests of the Company (or any of its subsidiaries or holding entities) are listed for quotation in the official list of an approved stock exchange (per s83A-45(2));
2. the Company was incorporated less than **10 years** before the end of the Company's most recent income year (per s83A-45(3));
3. the Company (together with all its subsidiaries and any predecessor entities) had aggregated turnover of **A$50 million or less** in the income year before the year of the Grant (per s83A-45(4)); and
4. the Company is an Australian resident taxpayer (per s83A-45(5)).

### 8.2 Interest conditions (per s83A-33)
The Board must further ensure that:
1. the Options are rights to acquire ordinary Shares (per s83A-33(2));
2. the Exercise Price is not less than the Fair Market Value of an ordinary Share on the Grant Date (per s83A-33(3));
3. the Options must be held for a minimum of **3 years** (or until earlier cessation of employment) before disposal (per s83A-33(1)(c)); and
4. the scheme rules are genuinely open to at least 75% of Australian-resident permanent employees who have completed 3 years of service (or the alternative test in s83A-33(1)(b) is satisfied).

### 8.3 Reporting
The Company must give each Participant an **ESS statement** by 14 July following the end of each income year in which an ESS deferred taxing point occurs (per s392-5 of Schedule 1 to the *Taxation Administration Act 1953* (Cth)), and must lodge an ESS annual report with the ATO by 14 August.

### 8.4 Written valuation record
The Company must retain contemporaneous written evidence of the Fair Market Value used to set the Exercise Price on each Grant, applying an approved safe-harbour valuation method under Regulation 83A-315.02.

### 8.5 s83A-105 election
Where a Grant does **not** qualify for the Div 83A Start-up Concession, a Participant may (where eligible) make a written election under s83A-105 of the ITAA97 to bring forward the taxing point to the income year of Grant. The Company must provide reasonable assistance to a Participant considering such an election, but does not provide tax advice.

### 8.6 No warranty
The Company does not warrant that any Grant qualifies, or will continue to qualify, for the Div 83A Start-up Concession. Participants are strongly encouraged to obtain independent tax advice.

## 9. Cessation of engagement (Leaver provisions)

### 9.1 Bad Leaver
If a Participant becomes a Bad Leaver:
1. all unvested Options **lapse immediately** without compensation; and
2. all Vested Options that have not been exercised also **lapse immediately** without compensation.

### 9.2 Good Leaver
If a Participant becomes a Good Leaver:
1. all unvested Options lapse on the date of cessation (subject to any Board discretion under clause 9.4); and
2. all Vested Options may be exercised at any time within the **90-day** period following the date of cessation, after which they lapse.

### 9.3 Death, TPD, or terminal illness
If a Participant ceases engagement due to death, Total and Permanent Disability, or terminal illness certified by a medical practitioner:
1. all Options vest immediately; and
2. Vested Options may be exercised by the Participant (or their legal personal representative) at any time within the **12-month** period following the date of cessation.

### 9.4 Board discretion
The Board may, in its absolute discretion, vary the treatment of unvested Options for any Good Leaver (including permitting continued vesting for a specified period). The Board may not use this discretion in a manner that jeopardises the availability of the Div 83A Start-up Concession for other Participants.

---

## 10. Change of Control

### 10.1 Single trigger variant
Where the Grant Letter specifies **single trigger** acceleration, all unvested Options vest immediately in full upon the completion of a Change of Control.

### 10.2 Double trigger variant (default)
Where the Grant Letter specifies **double trigger** acceleration (or is silent), unvested Options vest immediately in full upon the earlier of:
1. termination of the Participant's engagement by the Company or successor entity without cause within 12 months after a Change of Control; or
2. resignation by the Participant for Good Reason (materially reduced role, materially reduced remuneration, or relocation of the principal workplace by more than 50 km without consent) within 12 months after a Change of Control.

### 10.3 Treatment on the transaction
On a Change of Control, the Board must give reasonable notice (at least 15 Business Days) to Participants and may (with the consent of the acquirer):
1. procure that Vested Options are cashed out at the transaction price per Share less the Exercise Price;
2. procure that Options are rolled over into equivalent options in the acquirer entity; or
3. permit early exercise of Vested Options conditional on completion of the Change of Control.

### 10.4 Listing event
Where the Change of Control is an initial public offering, the Board may impose reasonable escrow or lock-up conditions on Shares issued on exercise, consistent with market practice and the ASX Listing Rules.

## 11. Administration

### 11.1 Plan administrator
The Plan is administered by the Board, which may delegate to a committee (typically the remuneration committee).

### 11.2 Board powers
The Board may:
1. determine the number of Options granted to each Participant and the applicable vesting and exercise terms;
2. determine Fair Market Value for the purposes of these Rules;
3. issue Grant Letters and make any consequential Board resolutions;
4. adjust the number of Options and the Exercise Price on a bonus issue, share split, share consolidation, capital reduction, or similar recapitalisation, in a manner that preserves the economic value of the Options;
5. establish sub-plans for foreign Participants; and
6. resolve any question arising in the interpretation or administration of the Plan.

### 11.3 No holding of Shares by the Company
The Company does not hold Options or Shares on trust for Participants prior to Grant or exercise.

---

## 12. Amendment and termination

### 12.1 Amendment by the Board
The Board may amend or repeal these Rules at any time, provided no amendment may adversely affect the accrued rights of an existing Participant in respect of a Grant made before the amendment, without the Participant's consent.

### 12.2 Shareholder approval
Any amendment that:
1. increases the size of the Reserve Pool above the level referenced in clause 3.1;
2. materially alters the eligibility conditions in clause 4;
3. reduces the Exercise Price of outstanding Options; or
4. extends the Expiry Date of outstanding Options,
requires the approval of the Shareholders by ordinary resolution.

### 12.3 Termination
The Board may terminate the Plan at any time. Termination does not affect the rights of Participants in respect of outstanding Grants.

---

## 13. General

### 13.1 No right to continued engagement
Nothing in these Rules or a Grant Letter confers on a Participant any right to continued engagement with a Group Member.

### 13.2 Rights not transferable
Options are personal to the Participant and cannot be sold, transferred, assigned, mortgaged, pledged, charged, or otherwise disposed of, except by operation of law on death.

### 13.3 Data privacy
Personal information collected by the Company in connection with the Plan is handled in accordance with the Company's privacy policy and the *Privacy Act 1988* (Cth).

### 13.4 Notices
Any notice under these Rules must be in writing and may be delivered personally, by post, or by email to the addresses last notified to the Company. Notices are deemed given as provided in the Company's constitution.

### 13.5 Governing law
These Rules are governed by the laws of New South Wales, Australia. Each Participant submits to the non-exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of New South Wales.

### 13.6 Disputes
Any dispute concerning the Plan or a Grant must first be referred to mediation administered by the Australian Disputes Centre. If unresolved within 30 days, the dispute may be referred to arbitration under the ACICA Arbitration Rules seated in Sydney.
