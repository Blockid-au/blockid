Secondary transactions typically trade at a discount to the most recent priced round to account for illiquidity.

## The Valuation Gap

| Metric | Priced Round (Primary) | Secondary Sale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Purpose** | Company Growth | Individual Liquidity |
| **Price Basis** | Future Potential | Current Fair Market Value (FMV) |
| **Dilution** | High (New Shares) | Zero (Existing Shares) |
| **Typical Discount** | 0% (Benchmark) | 20% – 40% |
| **Approval** | Board & Lead Investor | Board & Right of First Refusal (ROFR) |

## What to do about it

To set a fair price without triggering a full valuation event, follow this checklist:

*   **Apply Liquidity Discount:** Reduce the last round price by 20-30% for early employees.
*   **Check the SHA:** Review your Shareholders Agreement for Right of First Refusal (ROFR) clauses.
*   **Audit the Cap Table:** Ensure the sale doesn't create "dead equity" (large holdings by non-active members).
*   **Verify FMV:** Use a 409A-style valuation or SVI score to avoid tax surprises.
*   **Limit Volume:** Cap secondary sales at 5-10% of total equity to prevent signaling risk.
*   **Document the Trade:** Use a formal Share Transfer Agreement to maintain clean records.
