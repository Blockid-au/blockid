Institutional investors value "cleanliness" as much as growth.

## The "Red Flag" Audit
Check your registry against these common AU startup pitfalls.

| Issue | Risk Level | Investor Reaction | Fix Priority |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Dead Equity (Inactive Founders) | 🔴 Critical | "Why does a non-contributor own 20%?" | Immediate |
| Verbal Equity Promises | 🔴 Critical | Legal uncertainty; deal freeze | Immediate |
| No Vesting Schedule | 🟡 High | Fear of founder exit with full equity | High |
| Over-allocated Option Pool | 🟡 High | Dilution concerns for new lead | Medium |
| Unsigned Share Transfers | 🟢 Low | Administrative annoyance | Low |

## What to do about it
Fixing your equity now prevents valuation "haircuts" during your next round.

* **Audit every promise:** Convert every "I'll give you 2%" email into a legal agreement.
* **Reclaim dead equity:** Use buy-back agreements for founders who left early.
* **Implement cliff-vesting:** Standardise 4-year vesting with a 1-year cliff.
* **Formalise the ESOP:** Create a dedicated employee share option plan.
* **Update ASIC registries:** Ensure your share registry matches your internal spreadsheet.
* **Clean up "Friends & Family":** Convert early loans to convertible notes or equity.
