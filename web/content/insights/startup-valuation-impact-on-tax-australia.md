Setting a valuation too low to "save" employees money often triggers an immediate tax liability on the difference.

## The Cost of Undervaluing
When you issue shares below Market Value, the ATO views the discount as a taxable benefit (income), not a future capital gain.

| Scenario | Valuation Method | Tax Trigger | Primary Risk |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Nominal Value** | $0.01 / share | Immediate | Income tax on "benefit" |
| **ESIC Eligible** | Validated Startup | Deferred | Compliance audit |
| **Market Value** | SVI/DCF Model | Exit Event | Higher initial cost |
| **Aggressive** | Arbitrary Low | Immediate | ATO penalty interest |

## How to Protect Your Equity
Avoid the "equity trap" by aligning your issuance price with a defensible valuation methodology.

* **Document the "Why"**: Keep a valuation report contemporaneous to the share issue.
* **Use ESIC**: Leverage the Early Stage Innovation Company tax offsets to reduce risk.
* **Set Strike Prices**: Use options instead of direct shares to defer tax until exercise.
* **Audit the Gap**: Compare your internal valuation against recent seed rounds.
* **Track Cost Bases**: Record the exact value at issuance for future CGT calculations.
