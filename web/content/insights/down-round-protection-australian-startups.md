When new capital enters at a valuation lower than the previous round, anti-dilution triggers activate to protect early investors.

## The Anti-Dilution Trap

| Clause Type | Mechanic | Founder Impact | AU Prevalence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Full Ratchet** | Price resets to new low | Massive dilution | Rare (Aggressive) |
| **Weighted Average** | Blended price based on volume | Moderate dilution | Standard |
| **Pay-to-Play** | Forfeit rights if not investing | High pressure | Growing |
| **No Protection** | Common equity shares pain | Zero protection | Early-stage/Angels |

## How to Handle the Drop

Avoid "hope-based" valuations. Use hard data to find a floor that investors accept without wiping out the team.

* **Audit current KPIs** against AU sector benchmarks.
* **Propose a "Pay-to-Play"** to force inactive investors to contribute.
* **Recapitalise** by converting some preferred shares to common.
* **Increase employee options** to maintain talent morale.
* **Negotiate "Milestone-based"** valuation bumps for the next round.
