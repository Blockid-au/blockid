## How to justify your number

Investors ignore "comparables" if you can't prove your specific risk profile. Use hard data to defend your cap table.

*   **Audit your SVI Score** to quantify execution risk.
*   **Benchmark MRR growth** against sector medians.
*   **Map your runway** to a clear value-inflection point.
*   **Highlight proprietary IP** or "unfair" distribution channels.
*   **Align equity dilution** with standard AU seed rounds (15-25%).
