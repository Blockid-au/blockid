## Understanding Pre-Money Valuation
Pre-money valuation refers to your startup's worth before receiving an investment. It's a crucial metric for determining equity stakes and negotiating with investors.

## Key Factors Influencing Pre-Money Valuation
The following factors play a significant role in determining your pre-money valuation:

| Factor | Description | Weightage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Market Size | Total addressable market size and growth potential | High |
| Revenue Traction | Current revenue and growth rate | High |
| Competitive Landscape | Market competition and unique selling proposition | Medium |
| Team Experience | Founders' and key team members' relevant experience | Medium |
| Technology/ IP | Proprietary technology or intellectual property | Low-Medium |

## Calculating Pre-Money Valuation
While there's no one-size-fits-all formula, the following methods are commonly used:

- **Comparable Analysis**: Compare your startup to similar companies that have recently raised funds.
- **Discounted Cash Flow (DCF)**: Estimate future cash flows and discount them to present value.
