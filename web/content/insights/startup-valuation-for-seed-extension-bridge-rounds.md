Bridge rounds are meant to close the gap to Series A milestones, not reset your valuation floor.

## The Cost of the Bridge

| Method | Valuation Trigger | Dilution Risk | Speed to Close |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Flat Round** | Immediate | High | Medium |
| **Convertible Note** | Next Qualified Round | Deferred | Fast |
| **SAFE (AU)** | Next Qualified Round | Deferred | Fastest |
| **Down Round** | Immediate | Extreme | Slow |

## How to Price Your Extension

Avoid the "Flat Round" trap. Use a valuation cap that reflects your progress since the last seed round without over-committing.

* **Set a "Progress Cap"**: Price the cap 20-30% above your previous seed valuation.
* **Apply a Discount**: Offer 20% to early bridge investors to reward the risk.
* **Limit Total Raise**: Keep bridge funding under 20% of your original seed total.
* **Define the Milestone**: Tie the bridge to a specific KPI (e.g., A$50k MRR).
* **Check SVI Score**: Ensure your "investability" justifies the cap.
* **Model Dilution**: Run scenarios for the Series A conversion.
