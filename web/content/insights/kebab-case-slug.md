## Key Insights

In Australia, startup valuations can seem like a complex jungle. But fear not, here are some critical takeaways to help guide your valuation decisions:

### Startup Valuation Methods
#### 1. Scarcity effect
Startup success is a rare event in Australia, creating a scarcity effect that pushes valuations higher than comparable foreign startups.

#### 2. Growth potential
High growth potential is essential for securing funding and setting a realistic valuation. Investors benchmark against the AU benchmark for comparison.

#### 3. Market size
Understanding the size and potential of your market is crucial for setting a fair valuation. Knowing the benchmark is essential for negotiating a win-win deal.

#### 4. Competitor analysis
Learn from Australian peers and competitors to set realistic expectations for your valuation.

#### 5. Future revenue
Estimate future revenue to help guide your valuation. Startups with high future revenue have an advantage in negotiations.

## Hard Limits

STEP 1: What to avoid

* **Don't confuse growth potential with revenue potential.** Inferior startups may have high revenue but weak growth.
* **Don't ignore market size and global benchmarks.** Valuations depend on local AND global trends.
* **Avoid setting unrealistic revenue targets.** Revenue projection needs data-driven evidence and expertise.
