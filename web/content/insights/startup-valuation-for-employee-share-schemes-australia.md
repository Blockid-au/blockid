The ATO requires "Fair Market Value" (FMV) to ensure employees aren't receiving untaxed benefits.

## The Compliance Gap

| Valuation Method | ATO Acceptance | Risk Level | Best For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Last Round Price** | High | Low | Post-Seed / Series A |
| **DCF Model** | Medium | Medium | Revenue-generating |
| **SVI / Score-based** | Medium | Low | Pre-Revenue / Idea |
| **Arbitrary/Guess** | Zero | Critical | Nobody |

## What to do about it

To avoid penalties and protect your employees from immediate tax liabilities, follow this compliance checklist:

* **Document the "Why"**: Keep a dated memo explaining the valuation method used.
* **Use Independent Data**: Reference market benchmarks or third-party scores.
* **Avoid "Round Number" Bias**: $1.00 per share looks arbitrary; $0.94 looks calculated.
* **Sync with ESIC**: Ensure valuations align with Startup Concession requirements.
* **Update Annually**: FMV changes as you hit milestones; don't use 2022 prices in 2024.
* **Separate Option vs Share**: Value the underlying share, then apply the discount for options.
