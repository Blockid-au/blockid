The "Right Number" depends on which lever you are pulling: Team, Traction, or TAM.

## The Valuation Memo Framework

Stop putting a single number on a slide. Use a Valuation Memo to document the *why* behind the price.

| Component | Pre-Seed Focus | Seed/Series A Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Method** | Scorecard / Berkus | DCF / Multiples / Venture Capital Method |
| **Primary Driver** | Founder Pedigree & Market Size | MRR Growth & Churn Rate |
| **Risk Offset** | Technical Moat / IP | CAC:LTV Ratio / Unit Economics |
| **Benchmark** | AU Pre-Seed Medians | Sector-specific Revenue Multiples |

## How to Justify Your Number

Shift the conversation from "what you're worth" to "what the milestones justify."

* **Quantify the Moat:** List proprietary datasets, patents, or exclusive partnerships.
* **Map Milestones:** Show exactly how the investment unlocks the next valuation jump.
* **Use Local Benchmarks:** Reference current AU venture trends, not Silicon Valley outliers.
* **Address the "Haircut":** Acknowledge risks upfront to build investor trust.
* **SVI Alignment:** Use a standardized score to remove emotional bias.
