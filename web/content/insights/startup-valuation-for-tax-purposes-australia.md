Undocumented valuations trigger "deemed income" events, turning equity grants into immediate tax bills.

## The Cost of Undefended Valuations

| Event | Tax Trigger | ATO Requirement | Risk if Undocumented |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ESOP Issue** | Div 83A Income | Fair Market Value (FMV) | Immediate tax on "discount" |
| **Share Transfer** | CGT Event A1 | Cost Base vs. Market Value | Underpayment penalties |
| **Founder Vesting** | Div 83A / CGT | Documented Strike Price | Unexpected tax on "gain" |
| **Secondary Sale** | CGT Event | Comparable Evidence | Audit of all shareholders |

## How to Secure Your Valuation

To satisfy an ATO audit, you need a "defensible" paper trail that proves the valuation wasn't arbitrary.

- **Document the methodology** used (e.g., DCF, Scorecard, or Market Multiples).
- **Timestamp your valuation** immediately before any share issuance or transfer.
- **Use independent benchmarks** rather than internal projections alone.
- **Align ESOP strike prices** with the most recent priced funding round.
- **Keep a "Valuation Memo"** explaining the logic for the current share price.
- **Review CGT cost bases** annually to avoid calculation errors during exit.
