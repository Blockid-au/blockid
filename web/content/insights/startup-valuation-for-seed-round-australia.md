The transition from pre-seed to seed requires moving from "potential" to "provable" metrics.

## The Traction-Based Benchmark
VCs no longer fund "decks"; they fund growth curves. Use these AU-specific benchmarks to anchor your ask.

| Traction Level | Typical AU Valuation | Primary Driver | Ideal Dilution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **MVP / Beta** | A$2M – A$4M | Team Pedigree + LoIs | 15–25% |
| **Early Revenue** | A$4M – A$8M | MRR Growth Rate | 10–20% |
| **Proven PMF** | A$8M – A$15M+ | LTV/CAC Ratio | 10–15% |
| **Scale-Ready** | A$15M+ | Market Capture Speed | <15% |

## How to justify your number
Stop guessing. Use a data-backed framework to defend your valuation during the term sheet phase.

* **Quantify your SVI Score** to prove technical and market readiness.
* **Benchmark against 5-10** similar AU seed rounds from the last 12 months.
* **Map your "Value Inflection Points"**—exactly what milestones this capital unlocks.
* **Present a "Bottom-Up" forecast** based on actual acquisition costs, not top-down percentages.
* **Lead with a lead investor** to set the price before filling the rest of the round.
