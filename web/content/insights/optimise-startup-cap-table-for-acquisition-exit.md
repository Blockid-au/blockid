## Understanding Cap Table Optimisation

Optimising your cap table is crucial for maximising your startup's exit value. A well-managed cap table ensures that your equity structure is clear, transparent, and attractive to potential acquirers.

### Key Cap Table Components

| Component | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Shareholders | Founders, investors, employees, and other stakeholders |
| Share classes | Different types of shares with varying rights and privileges |
| Ownership percentages | Percentage of shares held by each shareholder |

## Practical Tips for Optimising Your Cap Table

* **Simplify your cap table**: Minimize the number of shareholders and share classes to reduce complexity
* **Regularly review and update**: Ensure your cap table reflects changes in ownership and share structure
