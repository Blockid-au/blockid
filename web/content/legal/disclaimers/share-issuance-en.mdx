# Share Issuance — Administrative Record Only

The share issuance workflow inside BlockID — including cap-table
updates, allotment records, ESOP grant entries, and generated
resolutions — is provided as an **administrative and record-keeping
tool only**. It is not a substitute for the statutory share register
maintained by your company, and it is not a lodgement with the
Australian Securities and Investments Commission (ASIC).

Under **s254X** of the *Corporations Act 2001* (Cth), the company must
lodge notice of any share issue with ASIC within 28 days after the
issue using **Form 484 (change to company details)**. That lodgement
obligation sits with the issuer. BlockID does not lodge Form 484 on
your behalf, does not update ASIC's registers, and does not warrant
that your Form 484 will be accepted if you submit one. You must confirm
lodgement through ASIC Connect, your registered ASIC agent, or your
company solicitor.

The company's **statutory share register** required under Part 2C.2 of
the Corporations Act (in particular **s169** and related sections) must
be maintained in full and kept at the registered office or another
notified address. The register held in BlockID is a mirror for internal
workflow; the legally authoritative register remains the one you or
your solicitor maintains under Part 2C.2. Where the two diverge, the
statutory register prevails.

Share issuances typically require a directors' resolution, may require
a shareholders' resolution (for example under pre-emptive-rights
provisions in the company constitution or shareholders' agreement),
and may trigger disclosure obligations under Chapter 6D. Before
finalising an issue you should confirm authority to issue, class
rights, pre-emptive-rights processes, and any offer-document
requirements with an Australian-qualified solicitor.

Auschain PTY LTD accepts no liability for missed ASIC lodgements,
incorrect share-register entries, breaches of pre-emptive rights, or
any other statutory or contractual failure arising from your use of
the BlockID share-issuance workflow.
