# General Information Only — Not Financial Product Advice

The valuation snapshots, Startup Viability Index (SVI) scores, cap-table
projections, dividend models, exit scenarios, and any other numeric or
narrative output produced by BlockID are provided as **general
information only**. They are not personal advice and are not a
recommendation to acquire, dispose of, hold, or vary any financial
product.

Auschain PTY LTD is **not** a licensed provider of financial product
advice under Chapter 7 of the *Corporations Act 2001* (Cth). We do not
hold, and are not required to hold, an Australian Financial Services
Licence because we do not provide personal advice, deal in financial
products, or operate a financial market. Nothing in a BlockID report
takes into account your objectives, financial situation, or particular
needs, as those terms are used in **s949A** of the Corporations Act.

Where our output touches on matters that could constitute a financial
product — for example, an equity issuance, an ESOP grant, a token
distribution, or a valuation used to support a fundraise — you must
treat that output as background information only. The
**s949A general advice warning** applies: you should consider whether
the information is appropriate for you having regard to your own
objectives, financial situation, and needs, and, before making any
decision, you should obtain the relevant Product Disclosure Statement,
offer document, or shareholders' agreement and consider it in full.

You should also obtain independent, licensed financial advice from an
adviser authorised under an AFSL, together with independent legal and
tax advice from an Australian-qualified solicitor and registered tax
agent. BlockID's automated outputs use assumptions, industry benchmarks,
and public data that may be incomplete, out of date, or incorrect for
your specific circumstances.

To the maximum extent permitted by law, Auschain PTY LTD, its
directors, employees, and contractors accept no liability for any loss,
damage, or cost suffered by any person acting or refraining from acting
on the basis of any BlockID output. Reliance is entirely at your own
risk.
