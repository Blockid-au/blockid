# Equity Offer — Regulatory Boundaries

Any offer of shares, convertible notes, SAFE instruments, or other
securities structured or documented through BlockID must be made in
compliance with Chapter 6D of the *Corporations Act 2001* (Cth). BlockID
is a workflow and record-keeping platform. It is not a licensed offer
platform, not a broker, and not a substitute for a properly prepared
disclosure document, offer information statement, or CSF offer document.

**Small-scale personal offers (s708 exemption).** If you rely on the
personal offers exemption in **s708** you must stay within all three
caps in any rolling twelve-month period: no more than **AU$2 million**
raised, no more than **20 investors** issued securities, and offers must
be personal in character (that is, made to persons likely to be
interested because of an existing personal, business, or professional
connection). BlockID cannot warrant that your offer stays within these
caps. Tracking issuances, counting offerees, and staying under the
rolling limit is your obligation as issuer.

**Wholesale gates (s708(8) and s708(11)).** Offers made only to
sophisticated investors under **s708(8)** (minimum AU$500,000 parcel or
qualified-accountant certificate showing net assets ≥ AU$2.5m or gross
income ≥ AU$250k per year over the last two financial years), or only
to professional investors under **s708(11)**, do not require a
disclosure document. You are responsible for collecting, verifying, and
retaining evidence of each investor's wholesale status **before** the
issue. See our separate *Wholesale investor warranty* disclaimer for
the certification you make when relying on this pathway.

**Crowd-sourced funding (s738G and RG 261).** A CSF offer under **Part
6D.3A** must be made through a CSF intermediary that holds an AFSL with
the appropriate authorisation. BlockID is not a CSF intermediary. The
statutory caps under **s738G**, as elaborated in ASIC Regulatory Guide
261, apply: an eligible CSF company may raise up to **AU$5 million per
12 months** via CSF, and each retail investor is capped at
**AU$10,000 per issuer per 12 months**. Cooling-off, risk warnings,
communication-facility rules, and offer-document content requirements
under Part 6D.3A must be complied with by the intermediary and issuer.

You should obtain independent Australian legal advice before making
any offer of securities, whether relying on s708, Part 6D.3A CSF, or a
full disclosure document. Nothing generated by BlockID is a substitute
for that advice, and use of our templates does not shift compliance
responsibility to Auschain.
