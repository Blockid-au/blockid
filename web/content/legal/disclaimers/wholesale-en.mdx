# Wholesale Investor Warranty

By ticking the "wholesale / sophisticated investor" gate in BlockID,
you warrant to Auschain PTY LTD and to the issuer whose offer you are
viewing that you satisfy the wholesale-client test in **s761G(7)** of
the *Corporations Act 2001* (Cth), and that you can produce, on
request, contemporaneous evidence of that status.

The **s761G(7) individual wealth test** requires a current certificate
from a **qualified accountant** (as defined in s88B) confirming that
you have **net assets of at least AU$2.5 million**, **or** a **gross
income of at least AU$250,000 per year in each of the last two
financial years**. The certificate must be dated within the two years
preceding the offer. If you tick the wholesale gate on behalf of a
company or trust that you control, you warrant the same test is met by
that entity, or that another wholesale limb applies (for example, the
professional-investor limb in s9 / s761G(7)(d) or the large-parcel
limb in s761GA).

You further warrant that: (a) you have read and understood the
regulatory boundaries in our *Equity offer* disclaimer; (b) you accept
that offers made to you as a wholesale client are made **without a
disclosure document** under Chapter 6D and without the retail
protections in Chapter 7 that would otherwise apply; (c) you are not
using the wholesale pathway to circumvent retail investor protections
on behalf of any retail person; and (d) the qualified-accountant
certificate (or other s761G(7) evidence) is held by you now and will
be produced to Auschain, the issuer, or ASIC on request.

Auschain PTY LTD **relies on this warranty** to enable your access to
wholesale-only offers and to wholesale-only surfaces of the BlockID
platform. If any element of the warranty is or becomes untrue, you
must immediately notify us at `compliance@blockid.au` and cease using
the wholesale surfaces. Providing a false warranty may constitute a
misleading or deceptive statement under **s1041H** of the Corporations
Act and **s18 of the Australian Consumer Law**, and may expose you to
civil liability to Auschain, the issuer, and third parties who relied
on your status.

This warranty is given afresh each time you access a wholesale-only
offer or surface. It survives termination of your BlockID account with
respect to the offers to which it applied while it was in force.
