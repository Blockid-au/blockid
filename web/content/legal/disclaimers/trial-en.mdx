# 7-Day Trial — Recurring Charge Authorisation

Your BlockID trial gives you full access to the selected paid tier for
**seven (7) calendar days** from the moment you start the trial. A
valid payment card is required to begin the trial. We do not charge
your card during the trial period itself; a small authorisation hold
may appear from your card issuer to confirm the card is valid, and
that hold will drop off in accordance with your issuer's timelines.

At the end of the 7-day trial, the subscription **automatically
converts** to a paid subscription on the tier and billing cycle you
selected, and your card will be charged at the plan price disclosed
at signup. You will then be charged on the same day of each subsequent
billing period (monthly or annual, as selected) until the subscription
is cancelled. This constitutes your express prior authorisation to
Auschain PTY LTD, via our payment processor, to initiate recurring
charges against the card on file.

This authorisation is provided under **s31 of the Australian Consumer
Law (ACL)** and Schedule 2 of the *Competition and Consumer Act 2010*
(Cth), which requires that any recurring or ongoing charge be
authorised by the consumer. By starting the trial you confirm you are
the authorised cardholder, you understand the trial converts
automatically, and you agree to be charged on the schedule described
at signup and on your subscription receipt.

You may cancel at any time before the end of the 7-day trial to avoid
being charged. Cancellation is available in **Account Settings →
Billing → Cancel Subscription**. Cancellation takes effect immediately
for the trial; for paid periods already charged, cancellation prevents
the next renewal but does not entitle you to a refund of the
already-charged period, save for any refund rights that arise under
the ACL consumer guarantees.

If a charge appears that you did not authorise, or if you are unable
to cancel through Account Settings, contact `billing@blockid.au`. Your
statutory rights under the ACL, including the consumer guarantees in
Part 3-2 of the ACL, are not affected by anything in this notice.
