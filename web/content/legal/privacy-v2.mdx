# Privacy Policy

**Auschain PTY LTD · ACN 659 615 111 · ABN 79 659 615 111 · Sydney NSW**

Auschain PTY LTD ("Auschain", "we", "us", "our") operates the BlockID
platform at `blockid.au`. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect,
hold, use, disclose, and protect personal information in accordance with
the *Privacy Act 1988* (Cth) (the "Privacy Act") and the **Australian
Privacy Principles (APPs 1–13)** in Schedule 1 of that Act.

This version 2.3 takes effect on **12 September 2026** and replaces all
prior versions, including the separate notice that was previously
published at `/privacy`. This page, `/legal/privacy`, is the single
canonical Privacy Policy for BlockID. Version 2.3 adds the investor
data-room confidentiality (NDA) acceptance record and engagement
telemetry released on 11 September 2026; version 2.2 added the Money
Finder, Founder Radar, evaluator, and investor-matching features (see the
changelog at the end of this page).

## 1. What we collect (APP 3, APP 5)

We only collect personal information reasonably necessary to provide the
Services. The categories we collect are:

- **Identity and contact data** — your name, email address, and where
  you choose to provide it, your phone number, role, and company.
- **Account and authentication data** — hashed password material, OAuth
  identifiers (Google, Microsoft, GitHub) where you sign in via a
  provider, session cookies, and multi-factor tokens.
- **Uploaded evidence** — pitch decks, financial models, cap tables, KYC
  documents, wholesale-investor certificates, and supporting artefacts
  you attach to Reports.
- **Cap-table and equity data** — shareholder names, share classes,
  option grants, vesting schedules, and valuation inputs you enter or
  import.
- **Billing and payment metadata** — the last four digits and brand of
  your payment card, billing address, GST status, and Stripe customer
  and subscription identifiers. We do **not** store full card numbers;
  those are handled by Stripe.
- **Product usage and device data** — pages visited, features used,
  approximate location derived from IP, browser and device fingerprint
  attributes, and log data (timestamps, request paths, response codes).
- **Communications** — support tickets, email correspondence, and
  in-product chat transcripts.
- **Money Finder intake** — the business facts you enter so we can match
  you to grants and programs (clause 2B): a short business description,
  state or territory, city, stage, industry, year incorporated, ABN or
  ACN, turnover, R&D spend, headcount, export intent, funding need, and
  — only if you tick them — founder demographic flags.
- **Startups entered by evaluators** — where an investor, advisor, or
  accelerator uses BlockID to assess a startup, the name, website, state,
  and notes they enter about that startup and its founders (clause 2C).
- **Investor preferences** — for investor accounts, the sectors, stages,
  geographies, cheque band, minimum score, and firm name you set, and
  whether you have chosen to be discoverable to founders (clause 2D).
- **Notification and calendar preferences** — your Money Radar email
  setting, your calendar-feed token, and your dashboard layout.
- **Investor data-room records** — where a founder shares a data room
  through an investor link: the confidentiality terms the investor
  accepted (version and a hash of the clause text), the email address the
  investor chooses to give when accepting, a salted hash of their network
  address and their browser family; and engagement telemetry — which
  sections were read and for how long, documents opened or downloaded, a
  salted hash of the network address and a truncated browser string. The
  founder sees who accepted and what was read; the investor's raw network
  address is never stored.

We collect this information directly from you where practical (APP 3.6).
Where a third party (for example an accountant, co-founder, or
integration partner) submits information about you, we ask them to
confirm they have authority to do so.

## 2. Why we use it (APP 6)

We use personal information to:

1. **Deliver the Services** — authenticate you, generate Reports, run
   valuations, host cap tables, process payments, and provide support.
2. **Improve the Services** — analyse aggregated usage, run experiments
   under your consent preferences, and diagnose faults.
3. **Communicate with you** — service announcements, security notices,
   billing correspondence, and — only where you have opted in —
   marketing.
4. **Meet legal and regulatory obligations** — respond to lawful
   requests, comply with the ACL, the *Corporations Act 2001* (Cth), the
   Privacy Act, and tax law.

We do not sell personal information.

### 2A. Your startup's data {#your-data}

Your data belongs to your startup. We store it so every report builds on your own evidence and the AI reasons on your case. Founder-consented access tiers control who sees what.

In practice this means the evidence you upload, the scores and reports
generated from it, and the history that accumulates over time are held
against your startup's workspace so that each new report, valuation, or
next-step plan is produced from that accumulating record rather than
from a blank prompt. Access by anyone other than the account holder —
an investor, mentor, advisor, or programme — is governed by the access
tier the founder has granted (see the Mentor Access Policy at
`/legal/mentor-access-policy`) and can be revoked by the founder at any
time.

### 2B. Money Finder — grants, programs, and Founder Radar {#money-finder}

**What we collect and why (APP 3, APP 5, APP 6).** Money Finder matches
your startup to Australian grants, R&D incentives, accelerators, and
programs. To do that we ask for the business facts listed in clause 1
under *Money Finder intake*. Every field is used for one purpose:
working out which grants and programs your startup is eligible for and
producing the Money Finder report, application drafts, and quarterly
analysis refresh you request. The intake is held against your project
(`project_grant_profiles`), and each report you generate is held against
your account (`funding_reports`).

**Demographic flags are optional and are used only for eligibility.**
Some grants are open only to women-led, First Nations-owned, regional,
or young-founder businesses. You may tick those flags so we can surface
those grants; you do not have to. A flag you tick is used only to test
eligibility rules — it is never used for advertising, for scoring, or to
rank you against other startups, and it is never shown to an investor or
evaluator. Where a flag reveals sensitive information (for example
racial or ethnic origin), we collect it only with your consent, which you
give by ticking the box and can withdraw at any time by clearing it in
**Workspace → Funding**.

**Guest purchases.** You can buy a Money Finder report for A$3 without an
account. For a guest purchase we collect your email address and your
intake, and Stripe collects your payment. We store the Stripe session and
payment identifiers so we can issue your tax invoice and avoid charging
you twice; we do **not** store your card number. Your report is emailed
to you as a link that contains a random access token — anyone who has
the link can open the report, so please keep it private. If you later
create an account with the same email address, the report is attached to
that account. You can ask us to disable a report link at any time.

**Founder Radar emails and how to opt out.** If your plan includes
Founder Radar, we keep a list of the grants and programs that match your
intake (`funding_matches`) and check it weekly. We email you when a
matched deadline is 30, 14, or 3 days away, when a matched program
pauses or reopens, and — at most twice — to remind you to complete your
intake if Radar has nothing to match against. These emails are a
separate category called **Money Radar**. You can switch them off at any
time from **Workspace → Notifications → Preferences** or by using the
unsubscribe link in any Radar email; in-app notifications are not
affected by that switch. Radar is time-boxed on some plans (for example
90 days with the Startup Package) and stops automatically when it ends.

**Calendar feed.** You may subscribe to your deadlines from your own
calendar application. The feed URL contains a secret token that is
unique to your account. The feed lists grant and program names and
dates only. Anyone who has the URL can read the feed, so treat it like a
password; you can mint a new token from **Workspace → Funding**, which
invalidates the old one.

**Application drafts and analysis refreshes.** When you ask us to draft
grant application answers or to refresh your analysis for a new quarter,
the draft or refresh is generated from your intake and your existing
evidence and held against your account. These are inputs to the AI
providers in clause 5A in the same way as any other Report.

**Public grant and program pages.** Our catalogue of grants and programs
is refreshed weekly by reading public government and program web pages.
No personal information is sent in that process.

### 2C. Startups entered by evaluators {#evaluator-data}

**What this covers.** Investors, advisors, and accelerators ("evaluators")
can use BlockID to assess a startup that is not yet a BlockID user. The
evaluator enters the startup's name, website, state, and notes, and can
run a Trust BizReport, a re-score, or a batch of scores across several
startups. That information may include personal information about the
startup's founders — for example names in a pitch deck or in the
evaluator's notes. We collect it indirectly, from the evaluator and from
publicly available information about the company, because it is not
practicable to collect it from the founder first (APP 3.6, APP 5.2).

**Purpose (APP 6).** We use evaluator-entered information for one
purpose: producing the evaluator's assessment of that startup. We do not
publish it, we do not list the startup in any public index because an
evaluator entered it, and we do not contact the founder unless the
evaluator invites them.

**What the evaluator must do.** By entering a startup, the evaluator
confirms they have a lawful basis to assess it — typically a pitch, an
application to their program, or a public listing — and agrees not to
enter sensitive information (health, ethnicity, religion, political
opinion, and the other categories in s6 of the Privacy Act) in their
notes.

**Founder claim and consent tiers.** An evaluator can invite the founder
by email. The founder receives a one-time claim link; when they claim
the startup, they see who is evaluating it, gain shared access to the
reports about it, and can bring their own evidence into the record. Each
evaluation carries a **consent tier** that controls what is visible in
each direction, using the same tiers as the Mentor Access Policy:
*attributed only* (the default — the evaluator's private assessment),
*reports shared* (the tier a claim sets — founder and evaluator see the
same reports), and *full mentor* (the founder has granted workspace
access). The founder can lower the tier at any time.

**If you are a founder and an evaluator has entered your startup.** You
may ask us at any time what an evaluator holds about you, ask us to
correct it, or ask us to remove it, whether or not you have a BlockID
account. Write to `privacy@blockid.au` or use `/contact?topic=legal`
with the startup name and, where you have one, the claim link. We will
confirm your connection to the startup, respond within 30 days, and —
where you ask for removal — delete the evaluator's record of your
startup and every report generated from it, and tell the evaluator that
we have done so.

**Batch scoring and program reports.** Where an accelerator scores a
cohort in one batch, the same rules apply to each startup in the batch.
Quarterly sponsor and LP reports generated from a batch contain scores
and rankings; they do not contain founder contact details.

### 2D. Investor discoverability {#investor-discoverability}

Investor accounts can opt in to being shown to Growth founders whose
startup matches their thesis. This is **off by default**. When you turn
it on, founders can see your display name, your firm name if you have
entered one, and the preferences you set — sectors, stages,
geographies, cheque band, and minimum score — together with the reasons
the match fired. Your email address is never shown to founders. A
founder who wants an introduction sends that request to our support
team, who pass it on to you; your contact details are not released
unless you choose to reply. You can turn discoverability off at any
time from your investor settings, which removes you from every
founder's list immediately.

## 4. Retention (APP 11.2)

We retain personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for
the purpose it was collected, subject to the following minimums and
maximums:

| Data class | Retention |
|---|---|
| Financial records (invoices, receipts, GST-relevant records) | **7 years** — s262A *Income Tax Assessment Act 1936*, s286 *Corporations Act 2001* |
| Cap-table and share-issuance records | Life of the entity + 7 years |
| Consent events and disclaimer acknowledgements | Life of the account + 7 years |
| Support tickets and correspondence | 3 years after case closure |
| Application, security, and access logs | **30 days** rolling |
| Deleted-account backups | Purged from cold storage within 90 days |
| Money Finder intake (`project_grant_profiles`), including any demographic flags | Life of the project, or on request; cleared immediately when you clear a flag |
| Money Finder reports, application drafts, and analysis refreshes (member) | Life of the account, or on request |
| Money Finder reports (guest, A$3) | **12 months** from purchase, then the access link is disabled and the email address removed; the Stripe payment record stays for the 7-year financial period, or on request |
| Founder Radar matches (`funding_matches`) | While Radar is active plus **90 days**, or on request |
| Radar and onboarding email records (`email_drips`) | **90 days** after the send date, or on request |
| Startups entered by evaluators, evaluation reports, and batches | Life of the evaluator's account, or until the evaluator deletes the record, or on request from the founder (clause 2C) |
| Evaluator invitation and claim tokens | Until claimed, or **90 days** after issue, whichever is first |
| Investor preferences and discoverability flag | Until you change them, or on request; the flag is cleared immediately when you opt out |
| Calendar-feed token | Until you mint a new one or delete your account |
| Dashboard layout and notification preferences | Life of the account |
| Investor data-room NDA acceptances (`data_room_nda_acceptances`) — version, clause hash, optional viewer email, hashed network address, browser family | Life of the data room + **7 years** — a consent record, kept with the room it protects; never swept while the room exists |
| Investor data-room engagement telemetry (`data_room_engagement`) — section, dwell, document name, hashed network address, truncated browser string | **12 months** from the event, then deleted |

When retention expires, we delete, de-identify, or securely archive the
records in accordance with APP 11.2. An automated retention sweep runs
**weekly** and applies the fixed periods in this table to the Money Finder,
Founder Radar, email, evaluator-invitation, and data-room engagement
records; it never removes records that an active subscription still relies
on, and it keeps the financial records and the NDA acceptance records for
the statutory period.

## 5. Disclosure to third parties {#third-parties}

We disclose personal information only to service providers that help us
run BlockID. Each is bound by its business or enterprise terms to use
the information solely to provide the service to us and to protect it
consistent with the APPs. Our current providers are:

- **Supabase** (self-hosted Auschain deployment) — primary database, storage, and authentication. Sydney, Australia (`ap-southeast-2`).
- **Stripe Payments Australia Pty Ltd** — card processing, subscription billing, and tax invoices. Australian processing entity; parent processing in the United States.
- **Cloudflare, Inc.** — DNS, DDoS mitigation, and web application firewall. United States headquarters, global edge.
- **Resend, Inc.** — transactional email delivery. United States.
- **AI inference providers** (clause 5A) — generation of Reports, scores, valuations, and translations.

### 5A. AI inference providers {#ai-providers}

Reports, Startup Value Index scores, valuation ranges, C-Level agent
reviews, and translations are generated by large language models. The
platform runs a **provider chain**: each request is sent to the first
provider in the chain with available capacity, and falls back to the
next if that provider is rate-limited, unavailable, or fails. The chain,
in the order the platform tries it, is:

- **Groq, Inc.** — United States.
- **Cerebras Systems, Inc.** — United States.
- **SambaNova Systems, Inc.** — United States.
- **DeepInfra, Inc.** — United States (used only when a paid key is configured).
- **Anthropic, PBC** — Claude models, United States (regional endpoints where available).
- **Ollama** — models run on infrastructure we operate; no third-party disclosure.
- **OpenRouter, Inc.** — United States; a routing service that forwards each request to one of several upstream model hosts under OpenRouter's terms.

Where a key is configured, **Google LLC** (United States) is also used
for report imagery and interface translation (Gemini) and, only after
you accept analytics cookies, for aggregated product analytics (Google
Analytics 4).

When a Report or score is generated, **prompts and the relevant input
data — including text extracted from documents you upload, the fields
you enter, and publicly available information about the company — may
be transmitted to one or more of these providers under their business
terms in order to generate the output.** We send only what is needed
for the request, do not send payment details, and do not send your
account password or authentication material. For Money Finder that
means your intake, including any demographic flag you have ticked, and
for evaluator reports it means the startup details and notes the
evaluator entered together with public information about the company. Each provider's own terms
govern how it handles a request once received; we review those terms
before adding a provider to the chain and publish material changes to
this list before they take effect.

## 6. Overseas disclosure (APP 8)

Where a provider in clause 5 stores or processes personal information
outside Australia, we take reasonable steps under APP 8.1 to ensure that
provider handles the information consistently with the APPs — including
contractual data-protection terms and, where available, adherence to
recognised international frameworks. By using BlockID you acknowledge
that some of your personal information will be disclosed to and
processed by recipients in the countries listed above.

For payments, Stripe may process your billing information in Australia
and the United States. For AI-assisted Reports — including Money Finder
reports, application drafts, and evaluator reports — prompts and relevant
input data may be transmitted to the AI inference providers listed in
clause 5A, most of which operate in the United States, to generate the
output. The public grant and program catalogue refresh in clause 2B
sends no personal information anywhere.

## 7. Security (APP 11.1) {#security}

We protect personal information with encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+),
encryption at rest for the database and object storage, role-based
access control, mandatory MFA for administrators, hash-chained audit
logging, and least-privilege service-role credentials. No security
measure is perfect; if we become aware of an incident that meets the
threshold for an **eligible data breach** under Part IIIC of the
Privacy Act, we will notify affected individuals and the Office of the
Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) as soon as practicable in
accordance with the Notifiable Data Breaches (NDB) scheme.

## 8. Your rights (APPs 12 and 13)

You may:

- Request **access** to the personal information we hold about you (APP
  12);
- Request **correction** of information that is inaccurate, out of date,
  incomplete, irrelevant, or misleading (APP 13);
- Withdraw a previously granted consent (this does not affect processing
  done before withdrawal); and
- Export a machine-readable copy of your account data from
  **Account → Export**;
- Switch off Money Radar emails (clause 2B), clear a demographic flag,
  or turn off investor discoverability (clause 2D) yourself, with
  immediate effect; and
- If you are a founder whose startup an evaluator has entered, ask what
  is held, ask for correction, or ask for removal, whether or not you
  have an account (clause 2C).

Send access, correction, or removal requests to `privacy@blockid.au`.
We will respond within **30 days** and do not charge a fee for
reasonable requests.

## 9. Complaints and the OAIC

If you believe we have breached the APPs or mishandled your personal
information, please contact `privacy@blockid.au` first so we can
investigate. We will acknowledge within 5 business days and aim to
substantively respond within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may lodge a complaint
with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner:

- Website: `www.oaic.gov.au`
