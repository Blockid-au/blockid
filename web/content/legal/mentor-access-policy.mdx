# Mentor Access Policy

**Auschain PTY LTD · ACN 659 615 111 · ABN 79 659 615 111 · Sydney NSW**

This Mentor Access Policy explains what a mentor sees when a founder
invites them into a BlockID startup workspace, how long that access
lasts, and how the founder can revoke it at any time. It supplements
the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) and the
[Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) — where anything here is inconsistent
with those documents, those documents prevail.

## 1. Access tiers

Founders choose one of three mentor access tiers when they send an
invite:

- **Read-only summary** — the mentor sees the startup's public profile,
  SVI band, and any Reports the founder has explicitly marked as
  shareable. No cap-table, no financial detail, no upload access.
- **Reports Shared** — everything above, plus any AI-generated Reports
  (SVI, valuation snapshot, C-Level briefings) the founder has attached
  to the invite. Cap-table and payment data remain hidden.
- **Full Mentor** — full read access to the workspace, including the
  cap-table, ESOP scaffolding, uploaded documents, and any Reports.
  The mentor cannot edit, delete, or export data in bulk, and cannot
  invite additional users.

A mentor never has billing access, cannot change the subscription
plan, and cannot see the founder's payment method.

## 2. Consent lifetime and revocation

Consent for **Reports Shared** and **Full Mentor** tiers expires
automatically **6 months** after the founder approves the invite. The
mentor is notified 14 days before expiry and must be re-invited to
regain access. **Read-only summary** access does not auto-expire but
can still be revoked at any time.

Founders can revoke a mentor's access immediately from **Dashboard →
Mentor Invites** at any time and for any reason. Revocation is
propagated to the mentor's session within 60 seconds and removes all
in-flight download tokens.

## 3. Data retention after revocation

When mentor access is revoked or expires:

- The mentor's ability to view any Reports, dashboards, or workspace
  data ends immediately.
- Any Reports the mentor downloaded to their own device before
  revocation remain under their control — Auschain cannot recall those
  files. The mentor is bound by the confidentiality clause in the
  Mentor Terms they accepted at invite time.
- We retain an audit record of the invite, approval, and revocation
  events for **7 years** in line with our record-keeping obligations.

## 4. Contact

Questions about mentor access can be sent to `support@blockid.au`. To
raise a privacy concern about a mentor's use of shared data, contact
`privacy@blockid.au` — we will respond within 30 days as required by
Australian Privacy Principle 12.
