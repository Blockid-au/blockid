## Method

**Phase 1 — Static analysis.** Enumerated every `page.tsx` / `route.ts` under
`web/src/app` (482 route files), converted `[slug]` segments to regex patterns,
then extracted every internal href from `web/src/**/*.{tsx,ts}` and every
markdown link from `web/content/**/*.md`. Any referenced path that didn't match
a real route file (static or dynamic) was flagged.

**Phase 2 — Live crawl.** `curl` against `https://blockid.au`, top 30 static
routes then one-hop from `/` and `/insights` (60 additional URLs). All 200.
