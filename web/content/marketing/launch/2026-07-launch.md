# BlockID v2.0 beta — 7-day trial, 4-segment plans, in-flow save offers

We shipped the v2.0 beta of BlockID.au today across two back-to-back releases (`v2.0.0-beta.4` + `v2.0.0-beta.5`). This is the milestone that closes out Phase 3 (conversion stack) and Phase 4 (legal + QA infrastructure), and it is the first version of the platform that is priced, gated, and audited the way we intend to run at general availability.

## What shipped this milestone

**Trial and entitlements.** A single 7-day trial now sits behind a segment-aware pricing surface — `SegmentTabs` renders 12 SKUs across founder / investor / advisor / accelerator. In-flow triggers drive upgrade prompts with a hard 1-per-session cap and a 24-hour cool-down per trigger, so the funnel never nags. Cancel and downgrade paths route through an `ExitSurvey` + `DowngradeOffer` layer that can offer a `COMEBACK30` coupon, a 30-day pause, or a book-a-call — every acceptance is written to `churn_events` for CFO reconciliation.

**Investor / Advisor / Accelerator workspaces.** Each of the three professional segments now has its own workspace sub-pages. Investors get filterable `dealflow`, a `watchlist` with notes, and a weekly `digest` timeline. Advisors get a client `roster` and an engagement `notes` timeline. Accelerators get a `cohort` founder grid and a 4-KPI quarterly report they can hand to LPs.

**Legal and audit trail.** We shipped `disclaimer_registry` — 10 canonical disclaimers × AU/GLOBAL, each seeded with a sha256 hash of its body, so downstream PDF and DOCX exports can prove which exact wording a customer saw. A `<withDisclaimer>` HOC, an ACL s31 `<AutoRenewNotice>`, and an APP-compliant `<PrivacyBanner>` are wired end-to-end. Terms of Service v2 and Privacy Policy v2 (MDX) landed with ACL non-excludable guarantees, APPs 1–13, NSW jurisdiction, and a 12-month liability cap.

**CFO admin dashboard.** `/admin/pricing-metrics` gives the operating team net revenue (last 30d), GST accrual, active + trialing subs, trial-to-paid conversion, and churn events. The v2.0.0-beta.5 sweep dropped a bogus "runway months" tile that was returning ≈1.67 regardless of ARR — we would rather ship four honest tiles than five confident ones.

## Why it matters

Three asks kept coming back from investor conversations on the v1 build. First, "let me try before I pay" — v2 answers that with the 7-day trial. Second, "one price does not fit four buyer types" — v2 answers that with 12 segment-scoped SKUs. Third, "if you are talking about equity and digital shares, prove the audit trail" — v2 answers that with the hashed disclaimer registry and the ToS/Privacy v2 that spell out the guarantees. Nothing about the digital-shares scaffold ships without a legal review pass; a grep-gate in CI blocks any change that touches `blockchain-sync` or `tokenization` without `legal_review_passed`.

## What we learned

Two reflections worth writing down.

The disclaimer registry is more useful than it sounded on the spec. Once every canonical disclaimer has a sha256 hash of its body, the consent chain stops being a promise and starts being a record — we can render the exact wording a user acknowledged, months later, without hoping the marketing copy has not been edited underneath us. The v2.0.0-beta.5 sweep caught a subtle bug here: migration `0080` originally used `ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE`, which would silently mutate stored hashes on a repeat run. It is now `DO NOTHING`. Consent-chain integrity is only as strong as its weakest re-seed.

Parallel-agent orchestration on strictly disjoint file domains cut the Phase 3–4 wall-clock from what would have been a multi-week sprint to a single working day. The rule that made it work: agents get non-overlapping write scopes, and every agent's output is committed and pushed before the next one is dispatched.

## What's next

- **Phase 6.** A `pick_lifecycle_due` Postgres RPC with `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` to eliminate the residual double-send risk in the lifecycle mailer and the `advance()` history race.
- **Phase 7.** An `axe-core` accessibility sweep across the marketing surface and the four workspaces.
- **v2.0 GA.** A 5-day staged rollout gated by `NEXT_PUBLIC_UPGRADE_V2`, with the CFO tile and the audit-chain runbook as the go/no-go signals at each ring.
